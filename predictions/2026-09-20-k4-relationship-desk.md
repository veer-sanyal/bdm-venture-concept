# PREDICTION - the RELATIONSHIP objection, desk half

**Written 2026-09-20 BEFORE dispatching four evidence lanes. Part 0: predictions land before results.
Mechanism first, rung number second (C-3).**

## The objection, as Veer put it

*"Maybe this doesn't exist because suppliers value the client relationship more than they lose from
chargebacks."*

This is a K4 SALIENCE hypothesis and a fourth candidate reason under C-53 (the three on file are:
asset cannot be assembled / a free actor covers the profitable half / nobody has a budget line).
`STATE.md` currently records supplier chargeback's emptiness as reason (i), the Ford PPGTC §20.01
pooling bar. If the relationship reading is true, reason (i) is not the binding one.

**It has two readings and they have opposite consequences:**
- **(a) Suppliers rarely dispute at all.** Kills the candidate. No disputes, no product.
- **(b) Suppliers dispute but ration it against relationship cost.** Does not kill it; specifies it.
  Triage becomes more valuable under a relationship constraint, not less.

**The desk cannot settle K4 (`STATE.md` line 435). It can bound it. A clean desk result does not clear
K4; it only fails to kill.**

## Predicted mechanism, per lane. Scored after results land.

**Lane A - supplier quality manuals, re-read for dispute consequence.**
PREDICT: manuals describe the dispute as a NEUTRAL administrative window with a deadline, and attach
NO explicit penalty to disputing. But they attach escalation (controlled shipping CS1/CS2, new business
hold, scorecard downgrade) to the UNDERLYING QUALITY EVENT, independent of whether the supplier disputes.
**If that is the shape, the relationship cost is priced to the DEFECT, not to the DISPUTE, and the
objection weakens materially: fighting adds no scorecard risk the supplier does not already carry.**
FALSIFIER, and it would confirm the objection from a desk: any clause conditioning scorecard status,
responsiveness rating, or new-business eligibility on DISPUTE ACTIVITY itself.
Confidence: moderate. This is the lane most likely to surprise me.

**Lane B - D3 state on the customer-side seat.**
PREDICT: **FILLED, TASK ABSENT** or **FILLED BUT INCENTIVE-INVERTED**, not FILLED AT THE CUSTOMER.
CQE/SQE postings will name containment, 8D, SCAR response, PPM reduction and customer satisfaction as the
stated PURPOSE. "Chargeback" appears in a minority of postings and mostly as "manage" or "minimize"
chargebacks, which is ambiguous between PREVENTING the defect and DISPUTING the debit.
**Specific call: fewer than 1 in 4 postings put disputing or recovering debits in the duties.**
**This is the lane I expect to hurt the candidate most, and it is not the lane the objection points at.**

**Lane C - D5 unprompted practitioner complaint, counted.**
PREDICT: complaints EXIST in volume (Elsmar Cove and equivalents) about padded or unjustified debits, and
a meaningful fraction of posters say they paid anyway. **Predicted dominant path, and this is the
mechanism prediction that matters: INFORMAL NEGOTIATION WITH THE BUYER'S SQE, below the level of a formal
written dispute.** If that is the real path, the objection is half right for a reason nobody stated: the
fight happens, but it is a phone call between two engineers, not a document package, and a product that
outputs a document package is aimed at a process that is not the live one.

**Lane D - D2 worse substitute.**
PREDICT: NO dedicated supplier-side automotive chargeback-defense consultancy found. Deduction and
chargeback recovery services DO exist in retail/CPG receivables, a different relationship structure.
If a supplier-side automotive one IS found, it is simultaneously D2 demand evidence and a K3 risk, and
must be scored as both.

## Overall
