# SCORING, batch A first two (screened 2026-09-07)

| Candidate | I predicted | Actual | Verdict right? | Rung right? |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GHS / SDS re-authoring | KILL at rung 4, mature SDS software market | **KILL at rung 4** - SDScribe at $1,595 with a landing page titled *"Contract blending and packaging"*; ExactSDS at $9,299/yr for 300 SDSs (~$31/SDS); 18+ named vendors; Enhesa publishes an *"SDS authoring software buyer's guide (2026)"* | **YES** | **YES** |
| Construction closeout, sub side | SURVIVES or one-more-check | **KILL at rung 3**, plus independent kills at rung 5 and on paperwork share | **NO** | n/a |

**1 of 2 on verdict. 1 of 1 on rung where the verdict was right.**

**Why I was wrong on closeout, precisely - this is the part worth keeping.** My reasoning was: "Pype and
Autodesk collect closeout docs GC-side FROM subs; authoring them sub-side is a different job and a
different buyer." **That framing is exactly the trap.** The GC does not merely own a competing tool - the
GC hands the sub a free seat in it and sends automated reminders when the sub is non-compliant. Procore:
*"Unlimited users, including not just your employees, but all other collaborators like clients,
subcontractors, and vendors"*, priced on the **GC's** annual construction volume. Autodesk Build:
*"Non-compliant subs automatically receive email reminders."*
**I treated "different buyer" as an opening. It was the reason the tool is free.**

**And two structural errors underneath it, both of which the ideation agent and I both missed:**
1. **The quoted instrument binds the wrong party.** UFGS 01 78 00 §3.2.1 is real and verbatim, but it
   runs against *"the Contractor"* under *"this contract"* - the federal PRIME contract. The word
   "subcontractor" appears **twice in 1,895 lines**, never in an obligation-bearing role. The
   Contracting Officer has no privity with the sub. **We named a checker who cannot check our customer.**
2. **The magnitude was denominated in money the product cannot move.** Retainage is released when the
   PROJECT reaches substantial completion, not when one sub's file is clean - *"still has to wait for
   framing, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, installation, drywall, and finishing subs."* A sub with a perfect
   same-day turnover package waits exactly as long as one without.
