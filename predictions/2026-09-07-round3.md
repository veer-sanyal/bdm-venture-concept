### ⚠ THE FINDING THAT MATTERS MORE THAN THE CANDIDATE

**The outcome this asset needs is never generated, and the industry already proved it.**

1. **The win is not attributable.** AGC of America, in its own 2019 program document: *"Often, insurance
   companies would rather pay a frivolous claim than fight one."* So a claim that resolves in the
   contractor's favour records the adjuster's settlement economics, not whether the four photographs
   worked. **The ledger would be full of rows whose cause is unknowable.**
2. **The collection experiment has already been run, on this exact population, for twenty years, and it
   failed.** CGA's DIRT is free, anonymous, and mandated in several states. AGC, same document:
   *"data input from professional excavators is relatively scarce compared to the amount of data
   provided by facility O/O's, locators, municipalities, regulatory agencies and 811 centers... By not
   doing so, others are telling the industry's story."* **The standard-setter cannot get excavators to
   report the LESS sensitive fact, for free, anonymously. The candidate needs the MORE sensitive one -
   what my insurer paid and why - reported to an undergraduate.**

**NEW STAGE-1 TEST, and it is the fifth asset test I predicted this round would produce - though not the
one I guessed.** I guessed the missing test was *"name the other party to the private record and ask what
they want."* The screens say the sharper question is:

> **1e. DOES THE OUTCOME COME BACK TO YOUR CUSTOMER, IN A FORM THEY CAN HAND YOU?**
> A private-by-operating asset assumes the graded result lands in the customer's lap. Often it does not.
> Ask three things: **who observes the outcome**, **is the cause attributable or is the result confounded
> by the observer's own economics**, and **has anyone already tried to collect this and failed?**

Run against this round's candidates it separates them immediately, which is what a good test does:
- **Utility damage: FAILS.** The insurer observes it, the cause is confounded by settlement economics,
  and CGA already tried and failed for twenty years.
- **Scrap grade calls: PASSES.** SDI notifies the yard *"prior to vehicle leaving site"*, with the reason.
- **PPAP: PASSES.** The Part Submission Warrant comes back to the supplier stamped, with a reason.
- **Supplier chargebacks: PASSES.** The debit memo and the disposition both land on the supplier.

**This test belongs in METHOD stage 1 and it should have existed before round 3, not after.**

## WHAT ROUND 3 ACTUALLY FOUND, and it is better than the rule I was hoping for

**Rung 5 killed two of four screened candidates, and for two DIFFERENT reasons, neither of which is what
METHOD's rung 5 currently asks.** Rung 5 today is one line: *can you reach the thing you act on, and does
the customer own the relationship.* Both kills passed that line and died anyway.

- **Utility damage claims: the outcome never comes back.** The claim settles between the utility and the
  contractor's insurer, and AGC's own document says carriers pay frivolous claims rather than fight them,
  so even a favourable result records the adjuster's economics, not whether the evidence worked.
- **PPAP: the outcome comes back, but may not be POOLED.** Stellantis/FCA eSupplierConnect Terms of Use:
  *"all Confidential Information, and including any and all other data that FCA and/or Company enter
  directly or indirectly into the Supplier Portal ('Data') remains the property of FCA"*, and
  *"Supplier agrees that it and each User may not use or disclose any Confidential Information other
  than for the express purpose of conducting Supplier's ordinary business of providing goods or services
  to FCA/Company."* Corroborated in Tenneco SRM §4.2 (which names *"reports"* and *"compilations"*
  explicitly) and Nemak SQM §1.2. Credentials are non-delegable and GM calls third-party portal access
  fraudulent.

**So rung 5 splits into three, and the third is a check nobody in this project has ever run:**

> **5a. Does the outcome come back to your customer at all?**
> **5b. Is it ATTRIBUTABLE - can you tell why it came back that way, or is the cause confounded by the
> observer's own economics?**
> **5c. May it be POOLED? Read the portal's Terms of Use and the supplier manual's confidentiality
