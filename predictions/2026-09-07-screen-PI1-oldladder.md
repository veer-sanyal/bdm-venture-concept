Corroborated by the vendor's own dated release: Predictive Analysis is designed to forecast whether a new lead or case "should be signed, referred out, or rejected", built using "proprietary data", trained on motor vehicle accident cases, with a named customer quote (Ryan McKeen, Connecticut Trial Firm) and "LeadsAI will be a paid feature add-on for Lead Docket customers" - https://www.filevine.com/news/filevine-announces-new-ai-powered-legal-intake-tool-leadsai-by-lead-docket/ (dateline October 17, 2023, Salt Lake City).

Scope of the kill, stated precisely so nobody over-reads it:
- **Claimed and shipped by the incumbent:** given the intake form, predict sign / refer-out / reject - for MVA leads only.
- **NOT claimed by any vendor found:** "improves as the firm's own case outcomes come back." The documentation says "machine learning model using lead data" and that the tag changes "as data is added or adjusted for the lead" (re-scoring on field edits, not learning from outcomes). "Proprietary data" is not attributed to the firm or to Filevine in the release text.
- **Why the unclaimed half does not rescue the candidate at this rung:** the same incumbent hosts the intake record (Lead Docket) and the case record through resolution (Filevine), so the asset PI-1 names - intake answers linked to what the case resolved for - already sits end-to-end inside the incumbent's own system. Closing that loop is a feature the incumbent adds to data it already holds; for an entrant it is the whole company. That is a distribution/feature gap on a timer, which rung 4 says "must never be the whole answer."
- Non-MVA case types are also unclaimed today; the release states planned expansion to "personal injury, mass tort, and class action litigation."

Tense/date check: release 2023-10-17 says "will be a paid feature"; help article updated 2025-09-09 says "is a paid feature." Live, current, present tense.

A mutation agent could test: same customer, asset = the firm's referred-out ledger (what each declined-and-referred lead resolved for), mistake = referring out a case the firm should have kept, or declining one it should have referred. Caveat that must travel with the lead: the Rule 1.5(e) mechanism and the 25-33% referral-fee range were reached only through search summaries (ABA rule pages 403'd); the forcing evidence is NOT VERIFIED at the primary. ## Stage 1-2 (run before the ladder)

- **Stage 1 asset, one sentence:** the firm's own intake transcripts and forms joined to each signed case's resolution and to what each declined lead did next. Category: private-by-operating, inside the customer's own documents. 1a: not public. 1b: outcomes, yes, for the signed branch; for the declined branch an outcome exists only if the lead was referred out and a fee came back. 1c: per-firm N at 3-30 attorneys is small; cross-firm pooling needs client consent (rung 2). 1f: observed, not computed - sign/decline is judgment over liability, coverage and damages, not arithmetic. 1e: observer of the signed outcome is the firm; attribution is confounded by the firm's own lawyering and the insurer's conduct; pooling is an absence of permission, not a foreclosure (rung 2).
- **Stage 2 mistake:** signing a case that will not pay, or losing one that would. Expensive: the acquisition cost of a signed case is priced by marketing vendors at "$2,000 and $5,000 for standard personal injury matters" in urban markets (Mohr Marketing, 2026-06-13, no external source cited - a vendor's benchmark, directional only). Gradeable fast: an experienced intake attorney grades a sign/decline call in minutes; that is the job. Frequent: continuous inbound volume. 2a: the money sits in the judgment, not the paperwork. 2a': the asset attaches to mistake half (a) (signed duds); half (b) (paying cases lost) returns a record only via referral - partial attach, flagged. 2b: the case is graded externally (adverse insurer, court) under a contingency fee; the DECISION is graded only by the firm's own P&L - passes narrowly, recorded. 2c: subscription, continuous. RATCHET pre-check: two outputs, no published conservative answer, both lawful - not a ratchet.
- **Stage 3 screens:** a16z four parts pass (fast expert grading, judgment matters, frequent, MVA-first then grow). Bessemer defensibility is the weak leg: the platform hosting the intake sees the same data.
