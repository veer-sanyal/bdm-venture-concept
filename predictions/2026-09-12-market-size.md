# Predictions: market sizing for supplier chargeback defense

Written 2026-09-12 BEFORE the three sizing agents return. Scored after, in this file.

## A. Supplier universe
- US firms in NAICS 3363 (motor vehicle parts), all sizes: 3,500 to 4,500. In a 20-499 band: 1,500 to 2,200.
- Indiana NAICS 3363 establishments: 250 to 350.
- The true Tier-2/Tier-3 universe (stampers, molders, machine shops classified in 332/326 selling into auto) is 2x to 4x the 3363 count, and NO primary source will attribute the auto share cleanly (70%).
- Appliance plus ag/construction machinery adds under 1,000 firms in band.

## B. Frequency and dollars
- At least three more Tier-1/OEM quality manuals with published chargeback fees will be found; admin fees $150 to $500 (80%).
- No credible published count of chargebacks per supplier per year will be found (80%).
- No credible published all-in dollar figure per chargeback (75%). If an anecdote is found, $2k to $15k typical, tail above $50k.
- The widely quoted OEM downtime cost per minute traces to vendor marketing, not a primary source (70%).

## C. Price anchors
- Recovery and deduction firms charge contingency of 20% to 40% of recovered (70%).
- SMB quality software runs $5k to $30k per year per site.

## The number
- Serviceable market for defense software in the US, 20-499 band, auto only: $30M to $150M a year. Criterion 6 stays at 2 unless frequency is far higher than guessed (65%).
