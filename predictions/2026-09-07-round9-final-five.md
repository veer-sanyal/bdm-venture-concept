## ⚠ THE REAL FINDING OF THE ROUND, AND IT IS ABOUT THE BOARD, NOT ABOUT ME

has fired as primary or contributory in FOUR candidates across four unrelated industries.** It is now the
dominant kill shape on the board, and the generalisation is cheap enough to run before anything else:

> **Most of these products have a conservative answer and a less-conservative answer. The conservative one
> is already free. The less-conservative one is the only one anyone would pay for - and it is the one the
> customer may not lawfully act on.**

Now written into `METHOD.md` at kill rule 6 as a stage-2 question: **which of my two outputs makes money,
and may my customer act on it?** A "no" there is cheaper than six rungs. **This is a consolidation of an
existing rule on four instances, not a new rule - I am still holding to not minting new ones today.**
