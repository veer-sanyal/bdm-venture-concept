## The rule bit, first, because it outlasts the candidates

**Stage 0c did its job on its first run: 17 rejected against 5 kept, and 0c itself killed four of the 17**
(franchisor royalty under-reporting, DCAA indirect-cost allowability, grain elevator inbound grades, and
the CPA-firm peer-review idea on 0c-ii). The same-cycle clause of stage 2 killed most of the rest. **A
filter that kills 4 of 17 on its own and shapes the remainder is biting, not decorating.**

### Two amendments the run forced, both now in METHOD

1. **0c-i sharpened from "does the customer possess the record" to "does the evidence of the mistake
   PHYSICALLY COME BACK to the customer's building."** The agent proposed it from having worked under the
   original and it is better: every one of the five survivors has physical arrival (a rejected pallet, a
   break report, a lien served, an audit letter, a driver's report), and every reject that died at 2b did
   not. **Same fifteen minutes, sharper cut.**
2. **0c-i has a side effect and must be run WITH 2b, never alone.** Pushing the grading record into the
   customer's hands pushes toward customers **whose only checker is themselves** - four rejects died there
   (bid-loss prediction, manufacturer-side warranty adjudication, distributor margin leakage, rental
   variants). **The hard box is record-in-hand AND an external checker.** The physical-arrival test is what
   reconciles the two: a pallet arrives *because* an external checker rejected it.

### The unflattering half, recorded because Part 9 says a rule that can only tighten is not calibration

**Three of the five survivors are physical-outcome candidates rather than paperwork candidates, which is a
different bet than the ENTIRE kill log makes.** That improves 2a a great deal - the money is materials,
machine time and trips rather than a document - and it makes **the cold start worse**, because a
private-by-operating ledger is empty on day one. **Criterion 7 buildability by 2026-12-11 gets HARDER, not
easier, and that is a real cost of the new filter, not a quibble.** Two of the five (R1, R2) are
documents-in-hand and do not pay this cost.
