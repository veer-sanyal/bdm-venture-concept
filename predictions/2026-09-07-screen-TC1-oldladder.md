## Stage 1 and stage 2 (METHOD Part 1), run before any search budget

**Asset in one sentence.** A per-contractor ledger of rework events labelled against the document state that
produced them (which revision was current, which RFIs were open, which coordination change was issued when).
Category: "inside the customer's own documents" plus private-by-operating.

- **1a** Not a public dataset. No yellow flag.
- **1b** OUTCOMES vs ADMINISTRATION: drawings, revisions, RFIs, change orders and daily logs are administration.
  The outcome (an install torn out and rebuilt) is not systematically recorded by a subcontractor as a labelled
  event; it surfaces as a punch item, a back-charge, or silently as foreman hours. The label the model needs is
  the one document the asset does not contain. NOT VERIFIED from any sample sub; flagged as the weak joint.
- **1c** Customer's own documents; coverage is by construction.
- **1d** Not applicable (no scattered-party assembly claimed).
- **1e** Observed by the sub itself (it does the demo and rebuild). Attribution is contested by construction
  (who pays turns on the cause). Pooling: see rung 2.
- **1f COMPUTED OR OBSERVED.** "Was this install built to the current revision" is DEDUCED from two documents the
  customer already holds (the current set and the set the crew used). No outcome needs to be observed to answer
  it, so the private-by-operating moat is unavailable for that half, exactly the C-71 shape. The only observed
  half is workmanship rework, which the asset never attached to (2a').
- **2a** Money sits in the physical act (tear-out labour and material), but the act is driven by an information
  failure, so the paperwork-becoming-free test is a partial pass: the wrong-revision and coordination slices
  disappear, the workmanship slice does not.
- **2a'** The mistake has three populations (wrong revision; missed coordination change; failed QC/inspection).
  The asset attaches to the first two. It does not attach to workmanship.
- **2b** Checkers named and quoted: the GC under A401-2017 §3.3.5 (*"The Contractor shall promptly notify the
  Subcontractor of any fault or defect in the Work under this Subcontract or nonconformity with the Subcontract
  Documents"*) and §3.5 (may *"remedy such default or neglect and withhold ... the reasonable cost thereof from
  current or future payments due the Subcontractor"*); the municipal inspector under a published re-inspection
  fee schedule (Wake County, below). Passes.
- **2c** Recurs per install, per job; several concurrent commercial jobs. Passes.
- **0c-i** The graded outcome physically arrives (the crew tears it out; the back-charge lands on the pay
  application). Passes.
- **0c-ii** A funded seat exists: Project Engineer / Assistant PM (see D3). Passes.
- **Stage-2 RATCHET question** ("which of my two outputs makes money, and may my customer lawfully act on it?").
  The conservative output ("verify and hold") is already the sub's contractual duty and is free: A401 §4.2.3.2
  (*"determined and verified materials, field measurements, and field construction criteria"*) and §5.1 (*"shall
  not thereafter ... perform Work that would be inconsistent with the changes"*). The paid output is
  prioritisation, which the sub may lawfully act on either way. RATCHET does not fire cleanly; recorded.
