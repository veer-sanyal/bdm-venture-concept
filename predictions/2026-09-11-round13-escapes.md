## ⚠⚠ THE STRUCTURAL FINDING OF ROUND 13, AND IT IS ABOUT METHOD PART 2c ITSELF

**ESCAPE 4 (the new scarcity above the raised floor) IS THE WEAKEST OF THE FOUR ESCAPES, AND IT IS WEAK
BY CONSTRUCTION.** Lane E ran the escape honestly and its own complement-test answers convicted it:
**6 of 8 candidates name a large incumbent sitting directly above the candidate**, and in most cases it is
**the same party that gave away the floor.**

- TASKPROOF -> "Deque sells paid audits above its free scanner and would extend downward into this."
- THE RECORD BELOW -> "JAVS, For The Record... gave away the transcript layer and will extend upward."
- MISSED -> "Epic gave away the floor and is the party best placed to extend upward."
- FIGURE CHECK -> "Clarivate and Elsevier both sit above this and both have shipped integrity tooling."
- PRACTICE EVIDENCE -> "they own the verification standard, so they can extend into this directly."
- CLAIM SUBSTANTIATION -> "Amazon can publish the offending-phrase list or build the check into the
  generator and end this overnight. Highest incumbent-extension risk of the eight."

**THE REASON, and it belongs in METHOD: the layer above a free floor IS STILL A COMPLEMENT TO THE FLOOR.**
Whoever gave the floor away wants the layer above it to work, because that is what makes the floor
valuable. So they extend upward. **Escape 4 does not escape the complement test. It relocates inside it.**
Escapes 1, 2 and 3 genuinely exit, because an adversary, a licence and an opposed interest are not
complements to anything.

**Only ONE of Lane E's eight had a clean answer: DECON CHAIN, "no incumbent is sitting directly above it."**
And notably it has nothing to do with AI.
