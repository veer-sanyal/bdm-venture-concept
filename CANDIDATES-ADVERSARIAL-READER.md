# LANE H - the adversarial reader (round 16 ideation, 2026-09-11)

**Ideation only. Nothing here is screened. No competitor sweep was run.**
Generated under `METHOD.md` Part 0 rule 1 (the DEAD list and DEAD CUSTOMER LANES were read first),
Stage 0b privity fast form, Stage 0c-i / 0c-ii / 0c-iii, Stage 1 asset tests, Stage 2 mistake tests,
Part 2b demand ladder, Part 2c complement test and the four escapes, and Stage 6 (AI last).

## The lane premise, restated as the generation filter

`EVIDENCE-AGENT-ECONOMY.md` established it: in law an adversary and a judge read the output before it
takes legal effect, which produced ~1,490 decisions worldwide and 1,000+ US sanctions in under three
years; in engineering, accountancy, medicine and customs brokerage the only reader before legal effect is
the licensee who seals it, which produced six licensing-board rules and ZERO enforcement cases.
**A board cannot discipline what nobody detects.**

> **GENERALISED: A MISTAKE IS PRICED ONLY WHERE A PAID OPPONENT READS THE SUBMISSION BEFORE IT BINDS.**

So every candidate below starts from **the reader**, never from an industry and never from a technology.
Each names: who is paid to disagree and the rule that makes disagreeing their function · what the
submission is and the moment it binds · what a "no" costs the customer.

**Evidence discipline (Part 7 rule 2):** every number carries a URL and the operative words, or it is
marked **NOT VERIFIED** plainly. Several are. A clean "not found" is left standing as a finding.

## H1 - THE AI LETTER (FDA 510(k) deficiency)

| field | content |
|---|---|
| **Name** | **The AI Letter** |
| **The adversarial reader** | The FDA lead reviewer in the relevant CDRH review division. Two rules make disagreeing their job, not their mood. (1) **The Refuse to Accept policy**: before substantive review, the reviewer runs a published checklist and the submission is administratively refused if any box fails. (2) **The Additional Information (AI) request**: under the MDUFA decision framework the reviewer places the submission on hold and issues an itemised deficiency letter, and the applicant's 180-day clock starts. The reviewer's performance is measured on the quality of the review, not on the applicant's speed to market - they are structurally paid to find the hole. |
| **Customer** | US medical device manufacturers filing Traditional and Special 510(k)s, **50-500 employees**, with a named Regulatory Affairs function but no in-house submission statistics group. Not the top 20 (they have submission-history databases and ex-FDA reviewers on staff). Size band NOT VERIFIED as a count. |
| **The mistake** | Filing a 510(k) that draws an RTA hold or a first-round AI letter on a deficiency the company could have pre-empted. Cost is not the FDA fee, it is **the calendar**: the review clock stops, the 180-day response window opens, and the launch slips a quarter or two while the sales forecast stands. Recurrence mechanism named: **new devices and device modifications**. A 510(k) is per-device, and 21 CFR 807.81(a)(3) forces a new submission on a change that could significantly affect safety or effectiveness - so the obligation recurs on the product roadmap, not on a renewal date, and additionally recurs *within* one submission because each AI round trip is another graded read. |
| **THE DIFFERENTIATED CLAIM, ONE SENTENCE** | Given this device's actual submission text and chosen predicate, we name the specific deficiencies **this review division** will raise in its first AI letter, before the submission is filed. |
| **The asset** | A corpus of **actual AI letters and RTA hold letters, with the filing that drew them**, keyed to review division and predicate family. FDA publishes 510(k) *decision summaries* and clearance dates; the deficiency letters go to the applicant and are not a published series. The asset exists because customers hand over the letter that arrived in their own inbox - private-by-operating, and cold-startable because every customer's own regulatory file already contains years of them (QSR record-retention makes that near-certain; the exact citation is NOT VERIFIED here). |
| **1. Complement test** | Nobody large. FDA has no revenue that this makes more valuable, and its own incentive runs the other way - it wants fewer bad submissions and publishes the RTA checklist free, which is a **PUBLISHES** risk under kill rule 6 rather than a complement risk. The closest commercial beneficiary is the regulatory consulting trade, which is services and fragmented. |
| **2. Privity** | **NO PRIVITY.** There is no contract between the manufacturer and FDA. No clause can foreclose pooling; the constraint on the corpus is the customer's own commercial secrecy, which their own agreement with us can carve. |
| **3. Physical arrival** | **YES.** The RTA hold letter and the AI letter arrive at the customer, through the FDA's electronic submission gateway and by email to the named correspondent, itemised deficiency by deficiency with the regulation or guidance cited. This is the cleanest form of 0c-i on the board: the graded verdict is a document addressed to the customer. |
| **4. Funded seat** | **Regulatory Affairs Manager / Director of Regulatory Affairs**, and below it **Regulatory Affairs Specialist**. This seat exists at a 50-person device company - it has to, because somebody must be the named correspondent. RAPS certification (RAC) exists for it. D3 state predicted **FILLED AT THE CUSTOMER**; not run here. |
| **5. Scales with customers, not hours** | The tenth customer costs an inference pass over their draft submission plus the ingest of their historical letters. The expensive part is the first corpus, not the tenth customer. The failure mode to watch is the pull toward doing the submission writing, which is hours; the product must stay the *prediction*, not the document. |
| **6. Market existence** | Money is actually charged here: 510(k) regulatory consulting is a transacted market with published rates, and MDUFA 510(k) user fees are published by FDA annually. Specific figures **NOT VERIFIED in this file** - no dollar is quoted forward. |
| **7. Non-competing members** | Scoped so it holds: pool **across review divisions and device families** (an orthopaedic implant maker and an in-vitro diagnostics maker do not compete). Within one division the members do compete, so same-division cross-pooling is excluded by design, not by hope. |
| **8. AI goes last** | This is an expensive, gradeable, repeating mistake first: the AI letter arrives, itemised, and an expert can tell in minutes whether we called it. **Given this submission's own text, its predicate choice and its testing summary, it decides which deficiency items this division will raise first** - a judgment a regulatory consultant is paid for today, wrong at a cost of a quarter of revenue, and gradeable the day the letter lands. It is a decision, not a draft: the output is a ranked list of predicted objections with the section that triggers each, not a written submission. |
| **My own strongest objection** | **FDA publishes the checklist.** The RTA checklist and dozens of device-specific guidances are free, and the conservative answer ("address everything the guidance lists") is free and unfalsifiable - which is METHOD's **RATCHET** shape: the saleable output is the *less* conservative one (skip this test, this predicate is fine), and a regulatory director may not act on a prediction against published guidance. Second objection: the number of 510(k)s per customer per year may be low single digits at this band, which puts stage 2c under real pressure even though the AI round trips add reads. |

## H2 - OBJECTION (state insurance rate and form filing)

| field | content |
|---|---|
| **Name** | **Objection** |
| **The adversarial reader** | The **state insurance department's product filing analyst or rate actuary**, reading a SERFF filing before the rate or form may be used. The rule that makes disagreeing their function is written into the filing system itself. NAIC's *Product Filing Review Handbook* describes the instrument: an objection letter "may ask the filer to revise a noncompliant form or rate filing or provide clarification or additional information, and will state the reason(s) for disapproval, including relevant case law, statutes and rules," and **"some states include a statement in the objection letter that until the deficiencies are remedied the filing is disapproved and may not be implemented"** (NAIC, Product Filing Review Handbook, https://content.naic.org/sites/default/files/inline-files/Product-Filing-Review-Handbook.pdf). Washington codifies the response duty: WAC 284-44A-090 requires a filer to **"Provide a complete response to an objection letter... a separate response to each objection"** and to **"Respond to the commissioner in a timely manner"** (https://www.law.cornell.edu/regulations/washington/WAC-284-44A-090). |
| **Customer** | US **small and mid-size P&C insurers, MGAs and program administrators** filing in multiple states - the band that files in 20-50 jurisdictions with a filing team of one to five. Not the top 10 carriers (in-house actuarial and filing departments, and outside counsel on retainer). Segment count NOT VERIFIED. |
| **The mistake** | Submitting a rate or form filing that draws an objection letter, then another, each round trip consuming 45 to 90 days of review clock while the rate sits unusable. The priced harm is **premium written at the old rate during the delay**, plus the filing analyst's and consulting actuary's hours per round. Recurrence mechanism named: **filings are per state per product per change**, and rate filings recur because loss trends move - a multi-state program writer is in the objection loop continuously, not annually. |
| **THE DIFFERENTIATED CLAIM, ONE SENTENCE** | Given this filing's own rate exhibits and form language, we name **which states will object and on what grounds**, before it is submitted to any of them. |
| **The asset** | The mapping from **filing content to objection ground, by state and by reviewer**. The unusual property, and it cuts both ways: in many states SERFF Filing Access publishes the correspondence, so the objection letters themselves are substantially **public** - which fails METHOD 1a (a great public asset is an already-mined asset) but **solves the cold start outright**: the training corpus can be assembled before the first customer exists. The private-by-operating layer on top is each customer's own submitted-but-not-yet-public drafts. Whether every state's objection correspondence is public is **NOT VERIFIED**. |
| **1. Complement test** | The nearest beneficiary is the **NAIC**, which operates SERFF and charges filing fees; a filing that clears faster marginally increases its throughput. NAIC is a standard-setter, not a commercial incumbent with core revenue to defend, so the exposure shape is **PUBLISHES** (it already publishes the Handbook free) rather than complement-commoditization. No large commercial incumbent's core revenue is made more valuable by this. |
| **2. Privity** | **NO PRIVITY with the reader.** The insurer has no contract with the state insurance department. There IS a contract with NAIC for SERFF access, whose terms of use are unread and are the real rung-2 question - but the reader itself cannot gag the customer. |
| **3. Physical arrival** | **YES.** The objection letter arrives in the customer's own SERFF workspace as a document, itemised objection by objection with the statute or rule cited, and the response letter is the customer's own filed reply. Both ends of the graded exchange sit in the customer's hands. |
| **4. Funded seat** | **Compliance Filing Analyst / Product Filing Manager / State Filings Specialist.** It exists at a 60-person MGA because somebody has to be in SERFF daily; the AIRC (Associate, Insurance Regulatory Compliance) designation exists for exactly this seat. Predicted D3 **FILLED AT THE CUSTOMER**; not run. |
| **5. Scales with customers, not hours** | Tenth customer costs an inference pass per filing. The danger is drifting into doing the filings (that is Perr&Knight's business and it is hours). |
| **6. Market existence** | Money is charged: states levy filing fees, and consulting actuaries sell objection-response work - Perr&Knight publishes an article titled "Best Practices for Managing Difficult DOI Rate Interrogatories" (https://www.perrknight.com/best-practices-doi-rate-interrogatories/), which is a vendor treating this exact pain as a named service line. Dollar amounts **NOT VERIFIED**. |
| **7. Non-competing members** | **NO MEMBER POOLING IS REQUIRED**, which is how this clears the withdrawn safe harbour cleanly. The corpus is public filings plus the customer's own history; insurers in the same line DO compete, so nothing confidential moves between them by design. |
| **8. AI goes last** | Expensive, gradeable, repeating first: the objection letter arrives itemised and an expert grades the prediction the day it lands. **Given a rate filing's exhibits and the forms attached to it, it decides which of the 50 states will object and on which ground** - a judgment a filing manager and a consulting actuary are paid for today. A decision, not a draft: the output is a per-state objection forecast, not a filled-in SERFF submission. |
| **My own strongest objection** | **If the objection letters are public in most states, the asset is buyable and the moat is execution, which METHOD names the FAKE ESCAPE ("they have not gotten to it yet").** Second: state insurance departments themselves publish filing guidance and checklists, so the conservative answer is free again (RATCHET), and a filing manager may not lawfully act on "this state probably will not notice." |

## H3 - REPAIR (SBA 7(a) guaranty purchase)

| field | content |
|---|---|
| **Name** | **Repair** |
| **The adversarial reader** | The **loan reviewer at SBA's National Guaranty Purchase Center**, Herndon VA, reading the lender's 10-tab purchase package after a borrower default and before SBA honours the guaranty. Their function is adverse by construction. Windsor Advantage, itself an SBA lender service provider, states the instrument plainly: **"A 'repair' should be viewed as a fine or penalty, reducing the SBA guaranty percentage, issued by the SBA during the purchase review process if the SBA determines the lender was imprudent"** (https://windsoradvantage.com/news/sba-loan-default-reasons-repair-denial). SBA OIG audited whether SBA was aggressive enough: it found final decisions on 16 of 32 reviewed loans made without sufficient evidence, **about $11.5 million in potential improper payments**, and 13 loans totalling about $5.4 million where SBA did not seek repair or denial because the statute of limitations expired (https://www.oversight.gov/reports/audit/sbas-7a-loan-guaranty-purchase-denial-review-process). **The OIG is pushing the reader to disagree more, not less.** |
| **Customer** | **Community banks, credit unions and non-bank 7(a) lenders** with an SBA portfolio but no dedicated SBA liquidation department - roughly the band that originates 10 to 200 7(a) loans a year. Count NOT VERIFIED. |
| **The mistake** | Servicing or originating a 7(a) loan in a way that hands SBA a repair or denial ground, and then discovering it only at purchase. The dollar is unusually clean: the mistake **reduces or eliminates the guaranteed portion of a specific loan**, so the harm is a percentage of a principal balance the lender already booked as recoverable. Recurrence mechanism named: **defaults**. A portfolio produces them continuously and independently of any renewal date, and every purchase package is a fresh graded submission. |
| **THE DIFFERENTIATED CLAIM, ONE SENTENCE** | Given this loan's actual file, we name the specific repair or denial grounds SBA will assert at purchase - **while the loan is still performing and the defect is still curable**. |
| **The asset** | A ledger of **actual repair and denial letters with the loan file that drew them**, keyed to defect type (unverified use of proceeds, intervening lien, missing collateral perfection, undocumented servicing action). SBA publishes loan-level origination data; it does not publish a series of repair letters against files. The letters arrive at the lender. The timing asymmetry is the whole product: the defect is created at origination or servicing and priced years later at purchase, so a model trained on what got repaired can be run at the moment the defect is still fixable. |
| **1. Complement test** | Nobody large. The beneficiaries of fewer repairs are the lenders themselves and SBA's own program-integrity mission. The closest commercial actors are **lender service providers** (Windsor Advantage and peers) and SBA-specialist law firms - services businesses, fragmented, and they monetise the defect rather than preventing it. |
| **2. Privity** | **NO PRIVITY with the reader in the gagging sense.** The lender's relationship with SBA is the guaranty agreement (SBA Form 750) and the SOPs, which impose duties on the lender rather than data restrictions on the lender's own file. The lender's own loan file is the lender's record. **Flag: the SOP and Form 750 have not been read here; the pooling question is a live rung-2 read, not a resolved one.** |
| **3. Physical arrival** | **YES.** SBA's repair or denial decision arrives at the lender as a written determination naming the ground, and the lender may appeal it. This is a graded verdict, itemised, addressed to the customer, with a dollar attached to each ground. |
| **4. Funded seat** | **SBA Loan Servicing Officer / Special Assets Officer / SBA Portfolio Manager**, and at larger lenders **Director of SBA Lending**. It exists at a community bank with an SBA department. Predicted D3 **FILLED AT THE CUSTOMER**; not run. |
| **5. Scales with customers, not hours** | Tenth customer is an ingest of their loan tape and document repository plus per-file inference. Repair grounds are common across lenders, so the corpus generalises; the per-customer work is integration, not judgment. |
| **6. Market existence** | **Verified that the money is actually disputed**: the OIG report above quantifies repair and denial decisions in dollars and criticises SBA for not pursuing them ($11.5M across 32 loans reviewed; $5.4M abandoned on the statute of limitations). Lender-side counsel sells repair defence as a named service (Starfield & Smith publishes on avoiding repairs, https://starfieldsmith.com/2019/06/simple-steps-to-avoid-intervening-liens-and-repairs-to-your-sba-loan-guaranty/). Per-customer annual magnitude **NOT VERIFIED**. |
| **7. Non-competing members** | **Real flag, stated plainly.** 7(a) lenders compete nationally, so cross-lender pooling of repair grounds sits in the conditioned lane. Mitigation by design: pool across lenders in **non-overlapping MSAs and non-overlapping industry concentrations**, and fall back to each customer's own history plus the published OIG and appeal record, which needs no exchange at all. |
| **8. AI goes last** | Expensive, gradeable, repeating first. **Given a 7(a) loan file's origination and servicing record, it decides which repair or denial ground SBA will assert at purchase** - the judgment SBA-specialist counsel is paid for, wrong at the cost of a slice of a guaranteed balance, gradeable the day the determination arrives. A decision, not a draft: the output is a ranked list of defect grounds with the curative action, not a written purchase package. |
| **My own strongest objection** | **SBA publishes the rules it will judge you against** - the SOP and the 10-tab checklist are free, and every SBA lender's counsel already teaches them. So the conservative answer is free (RATCHET), and the saleable answer, "this defect will not draw a repair, close the loan," is one a credit officer may not act on. Second: the interval between the defect and the grading is years, which is a cold-start and iteration-speed problem even under METHOD's verify-fast ruling. |

## H4 - PROVISO (BIS export licence applications)

| field | content |
|---|---|
| **Name** | **Proviso** |
| **The adversarial reader** | The **BIS licensing officer, plus the interagency reviewers at State, Defense and Energy** who read an export licence application before the export may lawfully occur. Disagreeing is the structure, not a mood: any reviewing agency may object, an unresolved objection escalates, and the application can come back **Returned Without Action (RWA)**, denied, or approved **with provisos** that change what the customer may actually ship, to whom, and under what conditions. The mechanism is in the EAR itself, 15 CFR Part 748 and Part 750 (https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-15/subtitle-B/chapter-VII/subchapter-C/part-748). **Scale of the adverse read: a 2021 snapshot reported 3,990 licences approved, 1,407 RWA'd and 526 denied - figures repeated by law-firm and think-tank secondary sources, NOT VERIFIED at a BIS primary page and not to be quoted forward.** |
| **Customer** | **US exporters and re-exporters of controlled items, 100-2,000 employees**, in semiconductors and semicap equipment, test and measurement, aerospace components, advanced materials and encryption-bearing software. The band with a compliance function but no former licensing officer on staff. Count NOT VERIFIED. |
| **The mistake** | Filing a licence application that comes back RWA or denied, or approved with a proviso nobody anticipated. The dollar is a **shipment that does not move on its forecast date** plus the entire re-application cycle; the worse version is shipping against a misread proviso, which is an enforcement exposure rather than a delay. Recurrence mechanism named: **per shipment, per end-user, per ECCN, per country**. An exporter with a controlled product line files continuously, and licences expire and are renewed, so the obligation recurs on the order book. |
| **THE DIFFERENTIATED CLAIM, ONE SENTENCE** | Given this ECCN, end-user, end-use and country combination, we name **the provisos this application will come back with, and the ground on which it will be RWA'd**, before it is filed. |
| **The asset** | A corpus of **actual licence outcomes with their proviso texts**, keyed to ECCN, destination, end-user type and licence type. BIS publishes aggregate statistics and the Entity List; it does not publish proviso language or RWA grounds against applications. Those arrive in the applicant's own SNAP-R account. Private-by-operating, and cold-startable from each customer's own licence history, which export-compliance recordkeeping obliges them to retain (15 CFR Part 762; exact retention period NOT VERIFIED here). |
| **1. Complement test** | Nobody large. Trade-compliance software (denied-party screening, classification) is sold by mid-cap vendors whose core product is screening, not licensing strategy; a proviso predictor makes their suites marginally more useful but is not a complement to any hyperscaler or dominant platform. BIS has no revenue at all. The government's incentive is the opposite of giving this away: it does not want the licensing threshold reverse-engineered. **This is the strongest complement-test answer of the eight.** |
| **2. Privity** | **NO PRIVITY.** No contract exists between the exporter and BIS or the interagency reviewers. Nothing can foreclose pooling. The real constraint is classification of the content, not contract law. |
| **3. Physical arrival** | **YES.** The RWA notice, the denial, and the licence with its provisos all arrive in the applicant's own SNAP-R account as documents, with the conditions itemised. The provisos are the graded verdict AND the operating instructions, which is why a misread one is expensive twice. |
| **4. Funded seat** | **Export Compliance Manager / Director of Global Trade Compliance / Trade Compliance Specialist.** It exists at a 150-person semicap supplier because the EAR makes somebody personally responsible. Predicted D3 **FILLED AT THE CUSTOMER**; not run. |
| **5. Scales with customers, not hours** | Tenth customer costs an ingest of their licence history plus per-application inference. Provisos are formulaic across applicants for the same ECCN/destination pair, so the corpus generalises strongly - this is the best scaling story of the eight. |
| **6. Market existence** | Money is actually charged: export-controls counsel and licensing consultants bill this work at published law-firm rates, and denied shipments are a line item exporters report. Specific dollar figures **NOT VERIFIED**. What IS established is that the adverse outcome is **counted** - RWAs and denials are reported in the thousands per year (secondary sources above, D4-shaped evidence at best). |
| **7. Non-competing members** | **CLEAN.** Pool across industries and destination profiles: an aerospace fastener exporter and an encryption-software exporter are not competitors. Same-ECCN direct competitors are excluded by design. No commercially sensitive price or output information is exchanged in any case; the shared object is government proviso language. |
| **8. AI goes last** | Expensive, gradeable, repeating first. **Given an ECCN, an end-user, a stated end-use and a destination, it decides which provisos the licence will carry and whether the application will be RWA'd** - a judgment export counsel is paid for, wrong at the cost of a shipment cycle or an enforcement exposure, gradeable the day the licence arrives. A decision, not a draft: the output is a predicted condition set, not a filled-in SNAP-R form. |
| **My own strongest objection** | **The graded outcome may be legally awkward to pool even without a contract**, because licence applications and outcomes can carry export-control-sensitive or classified-adjacent content, and a customer's counsel may refuse on that basis alone - that is a foreclosure with no clause behind it, which METHOD's privity test does not catch. Second: an exporter's whole compliance posture is conservatism, so the saleable "this will clear, ship it" answer is the one a compliance manager is least able to act on (RATCHET again). |

## H5 - COMMENT ROUND (franchise FDD state registration)

| field | content |
|---|---|
| **Name** | **Comment Round** |
| **The adversarial reader** | The **state franchise examiner** in a merit-review registration state, reading the FDD before the franchisor may lawfully offer or sell a franchise in that state. Disagreeing is the examiner's function: merit-review states issue **comment letters** that must be resolved before the registration is effective. Secondary but explicit: "In these states, your FDD must be registered with the state regulator - and renewed annually - before you may offer or sell a franchise there," and "Merit-review states like California, New York, and Maryland review substantively and issue comment letters that add time... a filing that draws multiple rounds of comments can take 30 to 60 days or longer" (https://www.franchiselawsolutions.com/learn/franchise-compliance/the-franchise-registration-states, https://thelawdept.com/franchise-laws-by-state/). **Law-firm sources: the registration-state list and the annual-renewal duty are NOT VERIFIED at any state statute in this file.** |
| **Customer** | **Emerging franchisors, roughly 10-250 units**, selling in multiple registration states, with a franchise development function and outside franchise counsel billed hourly. Not the 1,000-unit brands (in-house franchise legal departments). Count NOT VERIFIED. |
| **The mistake** | Filing an FDD or an annual renewal that draws comment rounds, while the **selling season is closed in that state**. The harm is directly monetary and simple to state: a franchisor that cannot sell in California or New York during its pipeline's peak loses franchise fees it had forecast, and pays counsel for every comment round at hourly rates. Recurrence mechanism named: **the statutory annual renewal in each registration state, plus a mandatory amendment on any material change** - reportedly ~13 states, so a multi-state franchisor faces dozens of graded submissions per year on a calendar it does not control. **This is the strongest 2c recurrence of the eight.** |
| **THE DIFFERENTIATED CLAIM, ONE SENTENCE** | Given this FDD's own item text and its blackline against last year's registration, we name **which states will comment and on which Item**, before any of them is filed. |
| **The asset** | A corpus of **examiner comment letters keyed to state, examiner and FDD Item number**, paired with the exact language that drew each comment. Some states make parts of the registration file publicly available; the comment correspondence as a series is not a published dataset (**NOT VERIFIED** either way, and the answer materially changes the moat). Private-by-operating, and cold-startable from a single franchise law firm's or franchisor's own file room, since every renewal preserves the prior year's correspondence. |
| **1. Complement test** | Nobody large. The beneficiaries are franchisors and the franchise bar; FRANdata and the franchise-law firms sell services around this and are fragmented. No platform's core revenue is made more valuable by faster FDD registration. |
| **2. Privity** | **NO PRIVITY.** No contract between the franchisor and the state regulator. Nothing forecloses pooling. (Note the inversion worth keeping: the franchisor is the *dominant* party in its own network - the franchise agreement - so METHOD's weak-party signature does not apply here at all.) |
| **3. Physical arrival** | **YES.** The comment letter arrives at the franchisor or its counsel as a document, itemised, citing the state rule and the FDD Item. The effectiveness notice or its absence is the second graded outcome. |
| **4. Funded seat** | **Director of Franchise Development**, and at the compliance end **Franchise Compliance Manager / Franchise Administrator**. Exists at a 60-unit franchisor. Predicted D3 **FILLED AT THE CUSTOMER**, with the risk that the task actually sits with outside counsel, which would read as **FILLED AT THE COUNTERPARTY-ish** and is the thing to check. Not run. |
| **5. Scales with customers, not hours** | Tenth customer costs an ingest of their FDD and last year's correspondence plus inference per state. The pull toward hours here is strong: FDD preparation is a legal service, so the product must stay strictly the comment forecast. |
| **6. Market existence** | Money is actually charged: registration states levy filing fees per state per year, and franchise counsel bills per comment round. Fee amounts and hourly rates **NOT VERIFIED**. The mistake's magnitude is denominated in lost franchise fees, which franchisors publish in Item 5 of the FDD itself - **that is an unusually accessible D1-adjacent number and the first thing a screen should fetch**. |
| **7. Non-competing members** | **CLEAN.** Pool across concepts: a pet-grooming franchisor and a quick-service taco franchisor are not competitors in any market. Same-category franchisors are excluded by design. |
| **8. AI goes last** | Expensive, gradeable, repeating first. **Given an FDD's Item text and the blackline against the prior registration, it decides which states will issue a comment letter and on which Item** - a judgment franchise counsel is paid hourly for, wrong at the cost of a closed selling season, gradeable the day the letter lands. A decision, not a draft: the output is a per-state comment forecast with the offending language highlighted, not a drafted FDD. |
| **My own strongest objection** | **The buyer may be the law firm, not the franchisor**, and franchise counsel's revenue comes from the comment rounds - which is METHOD's **INCENTIVE-INVERTED D3**: the seat that does this work is paid for the opposite answer. Second: if state registration files including correspondence are public, the asset is buyable and the moat collapses to execution. Third: ~13 states means the entire market is bounded by a fixed, small number of graded readers, so the ceiling on how much there is to learn is low. |

## H6 - SUMMARY STATEMENT (federal grant peer review)

| field | content |
|---|---|
| **Name** | **Summary Statement** |
| **The adversarial reader** | The **NIH study section reviewer**, whose assigned function is to find the weaknesses in an application before any money is obligated. The instrument is explicit and itemised: reviewers score 1-9 on **significance, investigator, innovation, approach and environment**, the application receives an **overall impact score**, and the applicant receives a **summary statement containing the assigned reviewers' critiques** - and applications judged roughly in the bottom half are **not discussed at all and receive no impact score**, only the criterion scores and critiques (https://grants.nih.gov/grants-process/review/first-level, https://www.niaid.nih.gov/grants-contracts/peer-review). The adversarial read is the whole institution: a study section exists to disagree, in writing, on the record, before the award binds. |
| **Customer** | Two bands, and they are different companies. (a) **Small research-intensive institutions and academic medical centres** with a Research Development office serving 50-500 PIs. (b) **SBIR/STTR-filing small businesses** (biotech, device, instrumentation), 5-100 employees, filing Phase I and Phase II proposals. Counts NOT VERIFIED. |
| **The mistake** | Submitting an application that draws a fatal critique on a fixable ground - and burning the **one permitted A1 resubmission**. # STRUCTURAL FINDINGS - not asked for, and the first one contradicts the lane premise

## 1. ⚠ THE LANE PREMISE IS THE WRONG GENERALISATION OF ITS OWN EVIDENCE. It is not the ADVERSARY. It is the PERMISSION.

I was told to generate from *"a paid opponent reads the submission before it binds."* Eight candidates came
out, and **every one of them has the same reader shape, which is not the shape I was given**: a **government
gatekeeper reading an application for permission**, who must say yes in writing before the customer may
lawfully act, and who must state a ground when it says no.

**And when I tried to generate from genuine two-party adversaries, the candidates died in my hands before
they could be written down:**

| Attempted, discarded | The reader | Why it collapsed at generation |
|---|---|---|
| Subrogation demand defence | The other carrier's adjuster | Two-sided buyer; and carriers compete, so the pool is in the conditioned lane |
| Surety bond declination | The surety underwriter | The bond agent coaches the WIP free because the surety pays their commission - kill rule 6 GIVES, at generation |
| Bid protest / debriefing | The contracting officer and rival bidders | Members compete by definition inside one solicitation |
| App Store / Play review rejection | The platform reviewer | **Fails the complement test outright**: the store's core revenue depends on apps shipping, so the platform publishes review guidance free. It is the complement-giver, not the adversary |
| Payment-network chargeback arbitration | The issuer | Network rules create privity, and the network already captures the mistake upstream |

**So the generative variable is not adversariality. It is that a permission must be granted before the
customer may act, and the grantor is obliged to write down why it refused.** Restated as a filter that
would have generated these eight faster and would have rejected the five above without any work:

> **A MISTAKE IS PRICED WHERE A GATEKEEPER MUST SAY YES IN WRITING BEFORE THE CUSTOMER MAY ACT, AND MUST
> STATE A GROUND WHEN IT SAYS NO.**

**This is a sharper filter than the one I was given, because four of the eight constraints fall out of it
automatically rather than having to be checked:**
- **Physical arrival (0c-i)** is guaranteed, because the refusal is a *required notice* addressed to the applicant.
- **No privity (0b)** is guaranteed, because nobody signs a data agreement with a licensor.
- **Non-competing members (constraint 7)** is usually satisfied, because applicants to one agency arrive from many industries.
- **A funded seat (0c-ii)** is near-guaranteed, because the instrument demands a named correspondent.

**None of those four falls out of "an adversary reads it."** The law evidence in
`EVIDENCE-AGENT-ECONOMY.md` fits the permission reading as well as the adversarial one - a court must
accept the filing before it has effect - so the 1,490 decisions do not distinguish the two hypotheses. **The
lane premise is not refuted; it is under-determined by its own evidence, and the permission reading is the
one that generated.**

## 2. ⚠ AND A SECOND READING OF THE SAME EVIDENCE THAT THE LANE PREMISE MISSES: DETECTION COST, NOT READERSHIP.

The premise says engineering produced zero cases because only the sealing licensee reads the work. There is
a competing explanation that fits the same facts: **a fabricated case citation is checkable in seconds by
anyone; an over-stressed beam in a sealed drawing is not checkable in seconds by anyone at all.** Law's 1,000+
sanctions may measure **detection cost**, not readership.

**This matters because it is testable and it reorders this lane.** Sort the eight candidates by how the
reader detects: H1 (RTA checklist), H2 (objection grounds against statute), H5 (comment letter against
state rule) and H7 (CF-28 question) are all **checklist detection, seconds cheap, and they are the four
with the tightest labels**. H3 (SBA repair, a judgment made years after the defect) is the weakest of the
eight on exactly this axis, and I noticed it while writing rather than by design. **Candidate strength in
this lane tracked detection cheapness, not adversary strength.**

## 3. ⚠⚠ RATCHET IS NOT A PER-CANDIDATE WEAKNESS IN THIS LANE. IT IS A PROPERTY OF THE LANE, AND IT FIRED ON SEVEN OF EIGHT UNPROMPTED.

I wrote "the strongest objection" field independently for each candidate and then noticed that **seven of
the eight objections are the same objection**: the gatekeeper publishes the standard it judges against, so
the conservative answer is already free, and the only saleable output is the *less* conservative one that a
compliance seat may not lawfully act on. FDA publishes the RTA checklist. Insurance departments publish
filing guidance. SBA publishes the SOP and the 10-tab list. BIS publishes the EAR. NIH publishes review
criteria. CBP publishes CROSS rulings.

**The mechanism is not coincidence and it is not laziness: a gatekeeper publishes its standard BECAUSE
publishing reduces its own workload.** Every permission-granting reader has that incentive. So METHOD's
RATCHET shape is **entailed** by the permission filter above, and any future candidate generated from it
inherits RATCHET before a single search is run.

**The one candidate where RATCHET does not fire is H8, and the reason is precise: its customer is the
CHALLENGER, not the duty-holder.** An intervenor wants the aggressive answer and may act on it; a
compliance manager wants the conservative one and may not.

> **RATCHET FIRES WHEN THE CUSTOMER IS THE DUTY-HOLDER. IT DOES NOT FIRE WHEN THE CUSTOMER IS THE
> CHALLENGER.**

**If that holds, it is a generator instruction and it points at exactly the escape METHOD says nobody has
generated in (Part 2c escape 3).** The lane that survives the free-competitor problem structurally is not
"predict the gatekeeper for the applicant"; it is **"predict the gatekeeper for the party attacking the
applicant."** Seven of my eight are on the wrong side of that line, and I only saw it after writing them.

## 4. The physical-arrival / frequency tension (C-70) IS resolved in this lane, by the round trip.

METHOD records as unresolved that *"the more consequential the checker, the less often it acts"* - an ICE
inspection, an IFTA audit at 3% of licensees, an IRS 226-J. **Permission gatekeepers break that tradeoff,
because the correction cycle is itself graded, repeatedly, inside a single submission.** An FDA AI letter, a
SERFF objection round, an FDD comment round and a CF-28 are all *multiple* graded reads of one submission,
and the submission itself recurs per device, per state per year, per entry. **So a consequential checker
that acts on every submission and several times per submission exists, and this lane is where it lives.**
That is a positive structural result and it is the strongest thing this lane has going for it.

## 5. The negative result, stated plainly because a clean not-found is a finding.

**I verified the READER and the INSTRUMENT cheaply for all eight** - the objection-letter duty (WAC
284-44A-090), the repair-as-penalty definition and the OIG's $11.5M, 19 U.S.C. 1514's 180 days, NIH's single
A1, the franchise comment-letter process, California's §§1801-1812 compensation standard. **I could not verify
a single per-customer annual DOLLAR from the desk for any of the eight.** Every magnitude line reads NOT
VERIFIED, and that is not laziness inside a 200-search budget - it is the fifteenth consecutive round in
which the readers, instruments and frequencies verify from a desk and criterion 3 does not.
**`STATE.md`'s standing read is unchanged and this round is another instance of it, not an exception to it.**
