## THE CASE FOR

- **Strongest rung-2 pass (K2 clean).** 49 U.S.C. 14706(a)(1): the carrier is "liable to the person entitled to recover under the receipt or bill of lading" for actual loss. 14706(f)(2): "Unless the carrier receives a waiver in writing... a carrier's maximum liability for household goods that are lost, damaged, destroyed... is an amount equal to the replacement value." 49 CFR 370.9: the carrier must "pay, decline, or make a firm compromise settlement offer in writing to the claimant within 120 days after receipt." 14708(a): the carrier "must agree to offer... arbitration." Every duty lands on the mover; it controls release of the money, so K1 is clean too.
- **Strongest demand tier: D1, a statutory price formula.** Replacement value by default; released value "$0.60 per pound for each item" only on the shipper's written waiver. Plus a fee-shift against the mover: 14708(d) awards the shipper attorney's fees if the mover failed to advise arbitration or decide within 60 days.
- **Best D2 number:** mover cargo insurance "Premiums typically run between 0.3% and 1% of the total declared cargo value," FVP deductibles "$0 to $500" (championrisk.com).
- **Rung 4 clean on the differentiated claim.**
