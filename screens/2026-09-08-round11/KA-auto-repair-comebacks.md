This warranty is not a warranty by National Automotive Parts Association (NAPA)"; "If you are less than 25 miles away from the original repairing NAPA AutoCare Dealer location, you must return your vehicle to the NAPA AutoCare Dealer location where the warranted service was performed." The duty-holder is the customer, and the instrument forces the comeback back to the shop that made the mistake (5a).

The counterparty prices in writing how much of a comeback it will absorb, and only for a part defect.
