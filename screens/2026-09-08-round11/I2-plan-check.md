# I2. Pre-submission plan-check correction prediction for 5-50 person architecture firms. Blind screen return, 2026-09-08.

Budget: 25 searches, 15 fetches, plus local text extraction of three PDFs.

## THE CASE FOR

- **D1, small but real, in the checker's own words.** Santa Clarita FY2024-25 Fee Schedule: "Recheck - 15% of Original fee per plan review after 3 reviews" and "S-001A MISCELLANEOUS/HOURLY PLAN CHECK $235.00" (caveat: the 15% line sits in the engineering/traffic block). 25 CCR §20 (HCD State Housing Law): "plans which have been returned to the plan submitter for correction shall be resubmitted along with a fee equal to 25 percent of the original plan check fee."
- **Strongest rung-2 pass: the instrument binds the customer.** B101-2017 template §3.1.5: the Architect "shall make, or cause to be made, revisions to the design and Construction Documents ... requested or required by governmental authorities ... as needed to obtain governmental approvals as part of its Basic Services." The correction letter arrives itemised: LADBS sheet, "Review corrections circled on this Plan Check Correction Sheet", "Italicized numbers refer to Code Sections of the 2020 Edition of the Los Angeles Codes", with "Plan Check Engineer: ____" on page 1. 5a and 5b clean.
- **Best D1/D2 number:** $235/hr (Santa Clarita). D2 anchors: Archidian "Get Started From $75"; UpCodes Plan Review "per-check pricing starting at $50" (NOT VERIFIED).
- **Rung 4 on the exact claim is clean.** No vendor claims to predict what a named jurisdiction's reviewer will write from the firm's own correction history. All five sell "check drawings against code plus local amendments."

## SCORED WEAKNESSES

- **Rung 1 (crit 3, 6):** the priced mistake is small and paid by the applicant, not the firm. The firm's loss is unbilled hours; no verified number. Archidian's "6 to 12 hours / set" is vendor marketing.
- **Rung 1.5 (crit 4):** US12045722B2, Design Group Partners LLC, granted 2024-07-23, Active: claim 1 covers receiving a design plan, obtaining "construction constraint and geographical constraint", identifying a "compliance threshold" and determining a "divergence element". Broad ML compliance-check claim; does not claim training on historical correction letters.
- **Rung 2 free appeal (crit 4):** LADBS Preliminary Plan Check, meet a plan check engineer before submittal, "$208.00" (NOT VERIFIED).
- **Rung 2 legislated-away (crit 7):** a dbs.lacity.gov document titled "Self-Certification Implementation Guide, Commercial Tenant Improvements" surfaced (title only). If LA removes plan check from TI, one named segment loses its mistake in the largest CA jurisdiction. Florida HB 683 (2025) authorising automated plan review, vendor-cited.
- **Rung 2 pooling:** not searched.
- **Rung 2.5 (crit 3):** D3 UNRUN at the band. D5 Archinect threads 403.
- **Rung 3 GIVES:** Archistar AI PreCheck given free to LA fire-rebuild applicants (NOT VERIFIED). Expediters at "$75/hour up to a few hundred dollars per hour" (NOT VERIFIED).
- **Rung 3 PUBLISHES (crit 4):** LADBS Commercial Plan Check Correction Sheets, 14 pages, ~140 numbered items, plus supplemental sheets. The checker publishes the entire correction universe; the reviewer circles.
- **Rung 3 CAPTURES (crit 4, 7):** city-side AI intake. Archistar, Austin: "roughly 50% time-savings from the zoning perspective"; LA County: "10 or 11 or 12" days to "five days or six days". CodeComply resold to cities by CivicPlus. When the city hands the applicant an AI report at intake, the mistake is captured upstream.
- **Rung 3 RATCHET (crit 5):** comply with every published sheet item is free; "this reviewer never flags X" cannot be acted on under B101 §2.2's Standard of Care.
- **Rung 3 UNDERCUT (crit 6):** UpCodes Plan Review, June 2026, "$50" per check, "6,000+ jurisdictions" (NOT VERIFIED).
- **Rung 4 (crit 4, 2):** five live vendors on the one-liner as the room hears it: Archistar ("Identify compliance issues before formal submission, reduce avoidable resubmissions"), CodeComply ("Catch code issues while you're still in the drawing", "Local amendment awareness", customers AHA Studios and Method Architecture, no outcome), PlanCheckPro ("improve first pass approval", "Over 650 firms"), Archidian, UpCodes. Buyer's guides exist. Exited predecessor: Fiatech AutoCodes, 2012 to ~2017 (NOT VERIFIED). The differentiated claim survives only by narrowing to reviewer-specific prediction, a weakness on criterion 2.
- **Rung 5c (crit 5):** a prediction the firm acts on is never labelled.
- **Rung 6 (crit 6):** AIA Firm Survey 2024: "more than 19,000" firms; 10-49 firms hold ~30% of billings (NOT VERIFIED). A per-firm asset at 5-50 people is thin.
