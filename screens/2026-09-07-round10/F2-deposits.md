## THE CASE FOR

**D1 reached, three statutes, quoted from the instrument.** CA Civ. Code §1950.5(m): bad-faith retention "may subject the landlord... to statutory damages of up to twice the amount of the security, in addition to actual damages" and "the landlord... shall have the burden of proof as to the reasonableness of the amounts claimed." TX Prop. Code §92.109(a): "$100, three times the portion of the deposit wrongfully withheld, and the tenant's reasonable attorney's fees"; §92.109(d): failure to itemize by day 30 "is presumed to have acted in bad faith." MA c.186 §15B(7): "three times the amount of such security deposit... plus interest at the rate of five per cent... together with court costs and reasonable attorney's fees." The checker's price for the mistake is written into law and the burden sits on the customer.

**Strongest rung-2 pass: K2 clean and the asset is now compelled into existence.** Every statute names the landlord (and successors/agents) as the duty-holder. CA §1950.5(g)(1)-(2), effective 4/1/2025 and 7/1/2025: "the landlord shall take photographs of the unit immediately before, or at the inception of, the tenancy" and again at move-out "prior to any repairs or cleanings for which the landlord will make a deduction," delivered with the statement under (h)(2)(D). The instrument was re-amended by AB 414 effective 1/1/2026, so every CA operator is rebuilding this workflow right now.

**Best number.** Per contested unit in CA: deposit capped at "one month's rent" (§1950.5(c)), exposure up to 2x that plus actual damages, in a forum where "you can't have a lawyer represent you" and filing costs "$30-$100" (selfhelp.courts.ca.gov). Frequency claim supported only by NOT VERIFIED turnover figures.

## MUTATION (M0)

The evidence does not force a new claim. It forces a **segment narrowing**: CA-first, because §1950.5(g) makes the asset exist by law from 2025 and small claims bars lawyers and fee-shifting, whereas TX and MA hand the tenant a fee-shifted lawyer. And it names the gap as **distribution, not capability**: Roost's own floor is 500 units. A pivot to selling adjudication to the absorbers (Rhino, LeaseLock) is conceivable but unsupported by any fetched page. No principled mutation of the claim itself.
