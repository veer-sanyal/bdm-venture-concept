## THE CASE FOR

Strongest demand tier: **D1, the checker's fee in writing.** Texas DOI: "The fee for a Tier 1 review is $650.00", "The fee for a Tier 2 review is $460.00", paid by "the carrier or the carrier's Utilization Review Agent" (tdi.texas.gov/hmo/mcqa/irofaqs.html, updated 9/13/2024). DOL TR 2011-02: "The cost of an independent review organization (IRO) to conduct an external review must be borne by the issuer (or plan)" and "There cannot be any restriction on the minimum dollar amount of a claim in order to be eligible for external review."

Strongest rung-2 pass: the penalty is real, binding and instrument-created. 45 CFR 147.136(d)(2)(iv): "Upon receipt of a notice of a final external review decision reversing the adverse benefit determination... the plan or issuer immediately must provide coverage or payment." 29 CFR 2560.503-1(l)(1): failure to follow procedures means "a claimant shall be deemed to have exhausted the administrative remedies available." Overturn rates are high where measured: Pennsylvania 1,353 referred, 655 overturned, "about 48%" since 2024 (pa.gov, 2026-04-03); Maryland "a 64% reversal rate" (KFF, 2021). The outcome comes back itemised to the file the customer holds (5a, 5b pass).

Best D3: Northwest Administrators (TPA, 411 employees) Appeals Specialist, "$30.70 - $41.54 per hour", duties "make informed appeal decisions" (builtin.com, removed 2025-09-26). D2: Allegiance ASA fee schedule, "A Claims Fiduciary Fee of $500.00 per case."

## KILL: K2 (the instrument does not bind the customer).

29 CFR 2560.503-1 places every duty on the "plan", "plan administrator" and "appropriate named fiduciary"; third-party administrators are not named. 45 CFR 147.136 binds "the plan or issuer" throughout. TR 2011-02: cost "borne by the issuer (or plan)". The one ASA read: "The Plan Sponsor delegates to the TPA only non-discretionary authority"; "Acknowledge that it is the Plan Sponsor, Plan Administrator, and Named Fiduciary." IRO fee, reversed claim, deemed exhaustion and 502(g) fees all land on the customer's customer.

Confidence caveat: the ASA is n=1, and the same ASA prices a "Claims Fiduciary Fee of $500.00 per case", so a TPA CAN become the duty-holder when the sponsor buys that. K2 stands on the regulations' text; the ASA shows the default posture.

## MUTATION (M0): forced.

The duty-holder who pays the IRO fee and the reversed claim is the plan sponsor, or a TPA acting as claims fiduciary for a per-case fee. Principled reformulation: same asset and document set, customer = the party holding discretionary appeal authority (the sponsor's appeals committee, or the TPA's claims-fiduciary desk billed per case). It inherits every rung-3 weakness (RATCHET, COMPELLED, CAPTURE by the UM vendor) and the 5c gap, so it is weaker, not rescued. Second mutation rejected: the deemed-exhaustion timing half needs a calendar.
