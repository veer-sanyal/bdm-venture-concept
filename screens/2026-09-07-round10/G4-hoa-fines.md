## MUTATION (M0)

The evidence forces a reconsideration but no principled mutation survives it. Forcing evidence: (1) the duty and the money sit with the association board and its counsel, who is already paid under fee-shift; (2) statutory fine caps make "do not fine" the free and rational default. Candidate mutations all collapse: selling the manager's own licence exposure ("$5,000 for each count") is a procedure log, which is what CINC's timeline module already is; moving the customer to association counsel makes it a law-firm tool for a party already reviewing for free-to-the-association. Answer: NONE that is not already an incumbent module or the lawyer.
