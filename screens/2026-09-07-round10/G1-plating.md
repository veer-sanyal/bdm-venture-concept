# G1. Pre-tank rejection prediction for US job-shop platers (20-250 employees). Blind screen return, 2026-09-07.

**Adjacency ruling.** Relative of the dead lane *private-by-operating assets sold to automotive Tier-2/3* and of *PPAP disposition history* (killed rung 5, pooling barred). Five-tuple differs on customer (diversified plater, not auto Tier-2), checker (prime receiving inspection under AS9100 8.6, not OEM PPGTC), instrument (Boeing GP7 §21 restricts "all materials derived... from the disclosing Party's Proprietary Information and Materials"; narrower than Ford §20.01's "aggregated, anonymized, or de-identified"), and the asset is single-shop, so pooling is not required by the formulation. Dead evidence transfers PARTIALLY: the shop's bath log is not derived from Buyer PI, but the NCR label is "Buyer-provided information pertaining to... quality testing" (GP7 §21a), so a pooled version reopens the PPAP question. The *scrap grade calls* lesson transfers fully: the gap is one dashboard on data the incumbent already holds.

## THE CASE FOR

- Strongest rung-2 pass (K2 clean, duty on the shop, label compelled back free): Boeing BCA 6xxx clause 6012, "Seller is responsible for the nonconformance(s) found in the article listed on this purchase document. The nonconformance will become part of Seller's quality rating." Lockheed Appendix QX rev 10 §1.14E: "Assess all Buyer-identified nonconformances, regardless if Item(s) was/were returned to Seller." QX §1.6A requires the shop to keep "all manufacturing, inspection, test, process capability... records" and "all nonconforming material, dispositions... assignable causes," so the asset is a record the shop is contractually required to already hold.
- Strongest demand tier reached: D3 signal (see state below) plus D5. No D1 or D2 verified.
- Best number: Census SUSB 2022, NAICS 332813, 513 firms at 20-99 employees with $3,261,022k receipts ($6.36M average) and 103 firms at 100-499 ($21.6M average); 616 firms in 20-499.
- Rung 1.5: no patent found reading on the claim. Nearest, US7124120B2 (Technic, expired 2024), predicts bath properties from voltammetry, not lot rejection.

**MUTATION (M0):** Forced. CAPTURES plus rung 5c show the standalone shape sits on data Steelhead and TrueChem already hold, and Elsyca already sells pre-tank geometry prediction. Two principled mutations: (a) narrow the mistake to salt-spray and adhesion failures, the destructive slow tests a shop cannot fully measure before release, leaving thickness to XRF; (b) change the customer to the chemistry supplier's technical-service arm, the GIVES actor whose field rep is the standing budget line. Both hinge on one unverified premise: whether in-spec baths produce a material share of rejections.
