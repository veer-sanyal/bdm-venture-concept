## THE CASE FOR

- Strongest demand tier: a partial D1. A live master spec (Odell+House 01 33 00, cawalker.net, filename dated July 2013) prices the mistake: "Architect's review of Contractors submittals is limited to review of an initial submittal and one resubmittal. The Owner will deduct from the Contract Sum amounts paid to the Architect for each additional re-submittal." Dollar amount unstated; lands on the prime.
- Strongest rung-2 pass: the outcome is contractually returned, itemised, to the submitter. UFGS 01 33 00 (Aug 2026): submittals "marked 'not approved,' 'disapproved,' or 'revise and resubmit' indicate incomplete submittal or noncompliance... Do not proceed with work for this item until the resubmittal is approved"; "The Contractor is responsible for additional time required for Government reviews resulting from required resubmittals. The review period for each resubmittal is the same as for the initial submittal"; "No delay damages or time extensions will be allowed for time lost in late submittals." UH master spec: "Allow 15 days for review of each resubmittal." So every rejection costs a verified 15-30 day cycle the sub cannot claim back.
- Best D2 number: a transacted price for the sub-side substitute exists, Submittal.app: Free $0 (3 packages/mo), Starter $25/seat/mo, Pro $149/mo, Team $349/mo (fetched).
- Rung 6: Census SUSB 2022 (fetched file): NAICS 238 firms at 20-299 employees = 42,774; Building Equipment Contractors (2382) = 19,252; glazing 755; finish carpentry 1,408. Countable and large.

## SCORED WEAKNESSES

- R0: every rejection-rate and cost figure in this space is vendor-sourced. BuildSync's "$805 per rejection" is its own survey; "30-40% first-round rejection" cites nothing. No D4 ledger exists. Criterion 3 (magnitude NOT VERIFIED, scores 1 until verified).
- R1: the mistake is denominated in TIME, not a fee; the only dollar price found (Odell+House deduction) is unstated and lands on the prime, reaching the sub only by back-charge. Criterion 5.
- R1.5: no patent reads on the claim. US8306924B2 (Submittal Exchange LLC, filed 2006-11-16) claim 1 is a four-tier access submittal log; no claim or abstract mentions predicting a review outcome. Clean.
- R2 duty-holder: every instrument read names the Contractor. UFGS: "The Contractor's [Quality Control Manager] must prepare, review and stamp submittals, including those provided by a subcontractor, before submittal to the Government." A201 §3.12.6 has the Contractor represent it has "reviewed and approved" (public copies; not fetched). The sub is bound only via the subcontract flowdown, which was NOT read. Criterion 7, and the heaviest R2 weakness.
- R2 free appeal: the first resubmittal is free by contract (Odell+House: initial plus one at no charge; UFGS: same review period, no fee). The penalty attaches to lateness and to the third try, not to the first rejection. NEMT shape. Criterion 3.
- R2 pooling: Procore SSA v2025.01.24 §5.1: Customer Data "owned exclusively by Customer" (the GC), with Procore licensed to compile "Aggregated Data"; User ToS v2.2 (2022-11-29) bars users from "Copy... distribute... or transmit for commercial... purposes" platform information. Not a foreclosure of the sub's own returned PDF, but the reviewer's comments are A/E work product on a GC platform. Criteria 4, 5.
- R2.5: does NOT clear. D1 partial, D2 present, D3 UNRUN, D4 not found, D5 UNRUN. Criterion 3.
- R3 GIVES: the GC's QC review of every sub submittal is mandatory and free (UFGS above); Submittal.app's free tier "scans your specifications to ensure every requirement is met before you submit." Removes the spec-compliance half. Residue: design-intent and reviewer-taste rejections. Criterion 4.
- R3 PUBLISHES: the spec IS the answer key. UH: "Incomplete submittals are unacceptable, will be considered nonresponsive, and will be returned for resubmittal without review."
