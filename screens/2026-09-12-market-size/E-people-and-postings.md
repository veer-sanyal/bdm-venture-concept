# Supplier quality chargebacks: counts and dollar amounts from people describing their own work

Scope: manufacturing quality chargebacks (automotive first, then appliance and heavy equipment), sourced from resumes, public profiles, job postings, theses/capstones, and trade press naming a real person or plant. Quality manuals, industry surveys, forums (Elsmar Cove), and vendor blogs were already searched in a prior pass and are not repeated here.

Searches run (not exhaustive, representative): resume-example sites (livecareer, qwikresume, cvcompiler, enhancv, mintresume, resumeworded, jobhero, velvetjobs, resumetrick, visualcv), postjobfree, Indeed resume and job listings, LinkedIn profile and Pulse snippets, Stellantis and other OEM career pages, ProQuest-adjacent university repositories (ScholarWorks WMU, OhioLINK, ResearchGate, IJFMR), AIAG Quality Summit pages, Quality Magazine, Automotive News, IndustryWeek, MOTOR magazine, appliance OEMs (Whirlpool, GE Appliances, Electrolux), and heavy equipment OEMs (Caterpillar, Deere, CNH). Practitioner vocabulary used throughout: supplier chargeback, SCB, debit memo, cost recovery, QPN, PRR, SMRR, NCMR, SCAR, quality spill, containment, CS1/CS2, sort charges, premium freight, line-down.

## What could not be found and why

1. **No real person's resume or public profile with a verifiable, directly-read chargeback count or dollar figure.** Every resume-site hit with a dollar figure attached to chargebacks or debit-memo recovery came from an explicitly-labeled example/template site (QwikResume, CVCompiler, Enhancv), not a real individual. The one apparently real, named profile found (Dennis Murphy, American Seating Company) could not be directly read: LinkedIn returned HTTP 999 on both the MCP-style web fetch and a direct curl attempt with a standard browser user agent, which is LinkedIn's anti-scraping block. Per the rule that a search-engine summary is not evidence, this was recorded as NOT VERIFIED rather than promoted to a confirmed data point.

2. **No job posting quantifying chargeback volume or a dollar recovery target.** Several real OEM postings (Stellantis's Supplier Recovery Project Manager, Warranty Recovery Procurement Specialist, and North America Cost Recovery Program Manager roles in Auburn Hills, MI) describe the function in qualitative terms ("maximizing cost-recovery performance," "strengthening the efficiency of warranty processes") but none state a number of chargebacks handled or a dollar target. Several posting URLs also returned 404/403/410 on direct fetch, meaning the exact current wording could not be independently confirmed even where a search snippet suggested relevant language.

3. **No thesis or capstone with plant-level chargeback counts or dollars.** Searches across ScholarWorks (WMU), OhioLINK, Wayne State, Purdue, Kettering, and Tennessee Tech repositories, plus ResearchGate and ScienceDirect, surfaced general cost-of-poor-quality and cost-of-quality case studies, but none reported chargeback claim counts, total dollars, or a derivable dollars-per-claim figure tied to an identified plant or company.

4. **No trade press or conference material naming a plant/manager with a specific chargeback figure.** The one on-topic hit with a real dollar figure (IndustryWeek/Graham Packaging, 2010) is a vendor case study for a BI product, outside the automotive/appliance/heavy equipment scope, describing projected savings rather than an actual chargeback total, and dated outside a useful recency window; it is documented above only to show it was checked and rejected. AIAG Quality Summit content is not searchable in archived slide form through general web search.

5. **Appliance and heavy equipment industries (the second and third priority) turned up nothing at all**, not even a near-miss like Dennis Murphy or the Stellantis postings. Searches for Whirlpool, GE Appliances, Electrolux, Caterpillar, Deere, and CNH combined with chargeback/recovery terms returned only company-overview and unrelated litigation results.

Overall result: this pass, like the first, found no verified count and no verified all-in dollar figure of supplier quality chargebacks from a person describing their own real work. The nearest candidates are flagged NOT VERIFIED above with the specific reason each could not be confirmed.
