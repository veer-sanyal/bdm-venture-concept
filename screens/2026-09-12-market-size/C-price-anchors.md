## What could not be found and why

- **The exact "$5.28 per claim manual" figure never surfaced.** iNymbus's own case-study page (fetched directly) states $5 per claim manual versus $1 per claim automated for the Warner Bros. example, an 80% reduction. No page, on iNymbus's own site or anywhere else in search results, uses the figure "$5.28." Treat that specific number as unfounded unless Veer has a source for it outside this search.
- **iNymbus does not publish price.** Both of its pricing-relevant pages were fetched directly and confirmed to contain no dollar figures; it is demo/consultation-gated.
- **Most QMS vendors serving small/mid manufacturers do not publish per-user price.** Of the eleven named (Plex, ETQ/Octave Reliance, Intellect, 1factory, Qualio, Ideagen, Uniwell, Harrington, QT9, Arena, MasterControl), only 1factory publishes real numbers on its own site. EASE and Supplios (supplier-portal/8D tools) also publish real numbers. The rest are "contact vendor," and the dollar estimates floating around search results for them (ETQ, Qualio, QT9, Arena, MasterControl) come from third-party aggregator sites that either blocked direct fetch (403: openregulatory.com twice, vendormint.com, utilisave.com, ziprecruiter.com) or, when fetched (Capterra for ETQ and Plex), turned out to show only "Contact vendor" rather than the numbers search snippets implied. I did not find any usable page for Uniwell or Harrington at all, and did not get to Tulip in this pass.
- **No third-party sort/inspection/containment firm (PLI, A&T/Quality Liaison, Global Quality Services, Team Quality Services, Stratosphere) publishes an hourly or day billing rate anywhere online.** This looks like standard industry practice (quote-only, negotiated per program). The one hourly number that does exist publicly, Stratosphere's $41/hour average via Salary.com, is what Stratosphere pays its own inspectors, not what it bills client manufacturers, and should not be used as a proxy for the service price without a large, unknown markup assumption.
- **No chargeback-dispute-specific consulting firm (Automotive Quality Consulting GmbH, Digital Automotive's Active Claim Management) publishes a fee structure.** Both sites were fetched directly and confirmed to contain no pricing language at all; they are lead-gen/demo-request pages.
- **BLS OEWS could not be read directly for Indiana Industrial Engineers (SOC 17-2112).** The BLS state index page is a table of download links, not inline data; the Indianapolis-metro regional news release (fetched, dated 2026-07-09) does not cover this occupation; and the Indiana Department of Workforce Development's own annual OES PDF is not machine-readable through the fetch tool used here (binary/compressed). The one number obtained, $98,550/year "Indiana Average Salary" from the state's own job-demand site (indemandjobs.dwd.in.gov), was fetched directly, but that page does not state its BLS OEWS vintage (which May-year survey) or whether it is mean or median, so it should be treated as directionally useful but not citation-grade against the specific BLS release requested.
- **No adjacent-category firm's contingency percentage was confirmed for telecom expense recovery or sales-tax reverse audits.** For telecom, the one page fetched directly (Auditel) confirms the contingency model in words but does not print a number. For sales tax, the Sales Tax Institute page (fetched) contains no fee data at all, and Cherry Bekaert's dedicated article on setting the contingent fee returned no content on fetch. The 20 to 30% (telecom) and 30 to 40%/up to 75% (sales tax) figures circulating in search results are therefore unconfirmed by any single source page and are excluded from the verified findings above.
