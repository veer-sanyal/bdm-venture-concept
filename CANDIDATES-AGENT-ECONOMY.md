## ⚠ CASEBOOK, THE PINCER (Drawdown Diary). A new kill shape, and it generalises.

**Where a remedy exists, a regulator runs it free. Where no remedy exists, your evidence buys nothing.**
There is no middle band where a private evidence product is both needed and useful. **Before building
evidence for a claim, check BOTH ends: is the claim already administered for free, and does the claim
exist at all in the jurisdictions without a program?**

## ⚠ CASEBOOK, THE IMPEACHING STUDY (Hum Line).

A free academic study on your exact question is worse than a competitor. **A pooled result that disagrees
with the published one does not win the argument, it gets impeached with it.** Check whether the question
has already been answered in public **and which way it came out** before assuming the answer helps you.
