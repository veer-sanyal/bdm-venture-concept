## 2. Patterns in how they started

1. **One document type × one well-paid professional × a daily task.** ~12 of 25 (Harvey, Legora, EvenUp, Eve, Abridge, Ambience, OpenEvidence, Hebbia, Basis, Crosby, Rilla, Granola). Counterexample: the fastest ARR ramps (Lovable, Replit, Perplexity, Suno, Manus, Genspark) were horizontal prosumer tools. Cursor is both.
2. **The market looked small, unglamorous, or already owned by an incumbent.** ~17 of 25 (plaintiff PI firms, HVAC sales, faxes; Copilot, Google, Nuance, Thomson Reuters). Counter: Sierra, Glean, Harvey went straight to prestige buyers.
3. **Founded before the model jump, repositioned when it arrived.** 8 of 25 (Glean, Clay, Replit, Abridge, EvenUp, Eve, Hebbia, Rilla) already had customers, data and integrations.
4. **Launched within ~6 months of a specific capability jump** (inference from dates): GPT-3/3.5 → Harvey, Perplexity; GPT-4 → Cursor, Claygent; speech-to-text + summaries → Abridge, Ambience, Rilla; long context → Hebbia Matrix, EvenUp, Eve, Legora; tool calling → Sierra, Decagon; agentic coding (mid-2024) → Replit Agent, Lovable; real-time voice → ElevenLabs, Vapi, Retell.
5. **Domain edge = "founder is the user" or "founder can reach the buyer", not credentials.** ~16 of 25. Those without it (Legora, Eve, Rilla, Mercor, Hebbia, Decagon) compensated with one design partner or fast market switching.
6. **Users redirected the wedge within 12 months.** ~8 of 25 (ElevenLabs, Cursor, Mercor, Perplexity, Abridge, Rilla, Clay, Hebbia).
7. **Priced per unit of work, then repriced upward.** ~10 of 25 (Sierra, Decagon, Mercor, Hippocratic, Crosby, Replit, Cursor, Rilla).
8. **Land one workflow, sell the next to the same buyer.** Almost all B2B.
9. **Consumer/prosumer path differs:** viral demo or open-source release → $10-25/mo card subscription → up-market. OpenEvidence is a hybrid (free to professionals, advertisers pay).
10. **Market story for vertical/agent companies was "the labor is huge"** (Foundation Capital's [$4.6T services-as-software](https://foundationcapital.com/the-4-6t-service-as-software-opportunity-lessons-from-year-one/) [S]; Rilla's "12 million salespeople" [O]); coding and consumer argued user count.

Hypothesis verdicts: (a) narrow frequent task for well-paid pro: holds for vertical B2B, not the fastest growers. (b) small/unglamorous first market: holds (~17/25) including "owned by incumbent". (c) labor budget: ~12/25; fails for coding and consumer. (d) domain access: weak as credentials; "user or buyer access" ~16/25. (e) capability jump: holds strongly. (f) consumer differs: holds. (g) labor-pool market story: holds for vertical/agent, not prosumer.

## 3. Commoditized or absorbed, 2023-2026

- **AI writing:** Jasper ($1.5B 2022) cut 2023 ARR forecast ≥30%, internal valuation −20%, CEO change ([Maginative](https://www.maginative.com/article/jasper-cuts-internal-valuation-as-ai-growth-slows/) [O]).
- **Chat-with-PDF, note-takers, enterprise search:** hit by ChatGPT file upload (Nov 2023), record mode and connectors (2025). Granola survived via team workspaces, $1.5B Mar 2026 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/25/granola-raises-125m-hits-1-5b-valuation-as-it-expands-from-meeting-notetaker-to-enterprise-ai-app/) [S]).
- **General chatbots / acqui-hires:** Character.AI (Google $2.7B license, Aug 2024; ~$50M revenue 2025 [S]), Inflection (Microsoft ~$650M), Adept (Amazon) ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2025/08/19/how-ai-zombie-deals-work-meta-google.html) [S]).
- **Coding:** Windsurf split between Google ($2.4B license/hire) and Cognition (Jul 2025) [S]; Cursor faced Claude Code ($2.5B+ run-rate Feb 2026) and Codex with thin margins, sold to SpaceX at $60B.
- **Scribes:** Epic launched AI Charting Feb 2026; Epic holds 42%+ of acute care ([Healthcare Dive](https://www.healthcaredive.com/news/epic-rolls-out-ai-charting-art-notetaking-documentation-scribe/811462/) [O]).
- **Hype without product/revenue:** Humane (HP $116M) [S]; Tome (20M users, <$4M ARR) [S]; Builder.ai bankrupt May 2025 [S]; 11x claimed customers it didn't have, 70-80% early churn ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/03/24/a16z-and-benchmark-backed-11x-has-been-claiming-customers-it-doesnt-have) [S]).

Survivors: inside the system of record; sell a finished outcome where raw ChatGPT fails on liability/regulation/accuracy; own a feedback loop and increasingly their own models; multiple workflows per buyer; honest ARR and retention.

## 4. Five tests for an early-stage idea

1. **Next-model test.** If the next frontier model shipped inside a generic chat app tomorrow, would the customer still need you? Pass: own the workflow step, the system-of-record integration, a liability/accuracy guarantee, or a proprietary feedback loop.
2. **Labor-line test.** Name the P&L line you replace; it should be ≥10x your price; price per unit of work.
3. **Frequency × pain × pay.** Daily task, top-three hated, for someone worth >$100/hr; usage shows without marketing.
4. **Capability-timing test.** Possible only in the last 6-12 months because of a nameable capability; improves automatically with the next model.
5. **Pull and second workflow.** Within 3-6 months: repurposing, inbound, or firm-wide rollout; an obvious second and third workflow for the same buyer; measured on retained revenue.
