# Judge, P4 (control: Definite), judge 1 of 3 — loop 2, 2026-09-25

Saved by the orchestrator from the judge's final reply. Scores: need 4, value 2, market 4, risk 2 (total 12). VERDICT: PASS.

Treated Definite (YC F26) as this team: its YC page matches almost word for word; its site now says "the ontology layer for finance"; no customer logos or pricing. An earlier YC launch pitched end-to-end bank regulatory reporting.

**Claims.** Heavy repeated work: supported (Federal Register 2025 estimates ~35–86 staff hours per quarter for one bank Call Report; 4,238 FDIC-insured institutions, Q2 2026; vendor-sponsored surveys on reconciliation time are weaker). Messy data needs engineers: partly true and easing (NetSuite AI Connector Service lets Claude/ChatGPT query the ledger). "Every size": weakest claim.

**Who else.** Incumbents own budget and channel: FloQast (3,500+ teams; Transform no-code natural-language agents; visual builder March 2026); BlackLine (4,400+ customers, $685M ARR; Verity agents). ERPs own the data (NetSuite reconciliation agent, AI bank matching; Oracle Ledger Agent). Funded "unified finance data" startups: Numeric ($89M, expanding to a unified data platform), Safebooks ($15M, read-only Financial Data Graph), Maximor ($9M), Zalos ($3.6M). Bank reporting channel: Fiserv, Jack Henry, FIS, Wolters Kluwer.

**1. Strongest version.** Drop "every size" and "build your own agents." Sell the compiler to the controller at a PE-backed, acquisition-built company with several ERPs, banks, processors and payrolls. First job: daily cross-system reconciliation with evidence; second: combined reporting for the PE owner; agents later. Sell via PE operating partners; keep read-only. Fallback: bank regulatory reporting.

**2. Ratings.** Need 4 (regulators estimate 35–86 hours/quarter per filing; recurring cross-system reconciliation cost). Value 2 (FloQast already sells natural-language agents to 3,500 teams; NetSuite lets AI read the ledger; only the multi-source compiler differs). Market 4 (BlackLine $685M ARR; Numeric, FloQast venture-scale). Risk 2 (close vendors own budget, ERPs own data, both moving onto this layer; read-only can't close the loop).

**3. Kill and test.** Kill: controllers won't pay for a separate data layer when their close tool or ERP ships agents; compiling matters only to companies messy enough to have data engineers. Test (~2 weeks): 10 controllers at multi-ERP PE portfolio companies already on FloQast/BlackLine/Numeric; offer a paid pilot on one uncovered cross-system reconciliation, done by hand from exports. Pass if ≥3 grant read access and sign or pay a deposit.
