The compiler with lineage explains every break; outputs drafted entries and workpapers for a human to post; read-only as a sales advantage next to BlackLine/FloQast; natural-language agents as the interface. Second vertical: credit union and community bank regulatory reports.

**2. Ratings.** Need 4 (Gartner). Value 2 (FloQast agents to 3,500 teams; ERP connectors standard; accuracy edge over Maximor/Numeric/Ledge unproven; read-only never finishes the work). Market 4 (BlackLine $700M from 4,394 customers; BLS 879,700 financial managers, median $166,570). Risk 2 (ERPs own data; close vendors own budget; per-customer connectors point to services-heavy).

**3. Kill and test.** Kill: the compiler needs custom engineering per customer, or FloQast/NetSuite-via-MCP is good enough. Test (~2 weeks, no product): raw GL, bank, processor and payroll exports from 3 payments-heavy mid-market companies; measure match rate with lineage and setup hours; ask for a paid pilot. Continue only if ≥95% match on every company, setup under 2 days each, and ≥1 signs.
