# Judge, P2 (candidate: quality paperwork), judge 1 of 3 — loop 2, 2026-09-25

Saved by the orchestrator from the judge's final reply. Scores: need 3, value 2, market 2, risk 2 (total 9). VERDICT: PASS.

**Bottom line.** Real, customer-mandated need, but low-cost AI tools already draft these documents, the $3K/month rests on human review hours, and the market is small: a good services business, not venture-scale.

**Checked.**
- Mandatory and flows down: IATF 16949 clause 8.3.4.4 → AIAG PPAP; GM requires sub-tier approval; TI Fluid Systems defaults to Level 3 and lists "Sub-Supplier PPAP"; Oshkosh 8D: containment in 24 hours, milestones at 15, 30, 45 days.
- Indiana wedge weaker than it looks: Toyota uses its own part approval process; Toyota, Subaru and Honda anchor Indiana, so many suppliers need customer-specific formats.
- Consultant pricing partly supported (forum: $1,400–$4,400 Level 3 cost; some shops $250–$500 per PPAP); no primary price sheet.
- "No quality engineer" plausible, unmeasured.
- Market modest: ~3,700–3,900 US IATF sites, mostly larger, falling since 2019; ~54,000 Indiana motor-vehicle-parts jobs → a few hundred target shops.

**Who else.** QualityEngineer.ai ($49–$249/seat/month; linked PFMEA, control plan, 8D; checks PPAPs; targets single-site suppliers; no human review — competitor, not this team); iFactory, Praxie; free/low-cost 8D generators (8D.wiki, IATF-Solutions); Omnex AQuA Pro and PPAPValidator.AI (mainly the reviewing side); 1factory; independent consultants, fractional QEs (SupplySourceSync), Purdue MEP training. No incumbent owns data or channel (CMM/inspection software, ERP, Excel; customer controls format and portal, not the purchase). No near-identical pitch.

**1. Strongest version.** Outsourced quality department for Tier-2/3 shops: lead with 8D complaint responses (recurring, deadlines, scorecard hits), PPAP as expansion; small retainer plus per-package fee; distribution through Tier-1 supplier quality engineers; per-customer format templates (AIAG, Toyota, GM/Ford); widen to aerospace FAI and medical.

**2. Ratings.** Need 3 (mandated with 24-hour to 45-day clocks; "owner does it" anecdotal). Value 2 ($36K/yr beats consultants only at ~10+ packages; AI drafting costs $49/seat; the added value is reviewer hours = services revenue). Market 2 (~3,800 IATF sites, mostly outside target; a few thousand shops at $36K ≈ $100–150M; Indiana a few hundred). Risk 2 (review labor caps margin; supplier signs the PSW; remote AI can't see the floor — a 2026 paper found none of 13 public compliance failures caught by routine document checks; AI tools push prices down).

**3. Kill and test.** Kill: too few PPAPs/8Ds per shop; owners won't outsource root cause to someone off the floor; review hours keep margins from software levels. Test: call 30 Indiana Tier-2/3 owners or quality leads (IATF portal or Purdue MEP); ask 12-month PPAP and 8D counts and spend; offer the next one as a paid $1,500 pilot; kill if median under ~10 packages/yr or fewer than 3 pay within two weeks.
