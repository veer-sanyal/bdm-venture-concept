## Key claims against primary sources
- Marlin-2B released on Hugging Face May 20, 2026, Apache-2.0, fine-tuned from Qwen3.5-2B; training data (~400K clips from ActivityNet, LSMDC, Charades) generated by Gemini-3-Flash in thinking mode and reviewed by people.
- "Matches a leading commercial model" is overstated: compared against Gemini-2.5-Flash in non-thinking mode (superseded); captioning within 0.21 and 0.43 points of 10; grounding "matches Gemini-2.0-Flash"; academic benchmarks only.
- "Fraction of the cost" has no figure; card says it runs on one consumer GPU.
- Cost problem real but smaller: ~100 tokens/sec of video; at $0.30/M (2.5 Flash) ~$0.11/hour, ~$950/camera/yr around the clock; Flash-Lite batch at $0.05/M ~$0.02/hour, ~$160/camera/yr.
- Gemini API terms forbid using the service "to develop models that compete" with it.

## Who else serves this customer
- Camera/VMS vendors own security's data and channel: Verkada (30,000+ orgs, $5.8B) sells natural-language search; Milestone built its own VLM on NVIDIA Cosmos Reason; Genetec, Motorola/Avigilon sell through installers.
- NVIDIA gives away Cosmos Reason 2, cookbooks and a video search/summarization blueprint (packaged by ClearML on-prem).
- TwelveLabs offers air-gapped on-prem; Moondream (open 1.8B, $4.5M) sells Lens fine-tuning; Conntour, Spot AI, Coram sell to this buyer.

## 1. Strongest version
Sell distillation-and-serving to companies already running VLMs over video at scale with a monthly frontier bill: video-analytics and security software vendors, physical-AI/robotics teams, data-curation pipelines. Ship a 1–3B model that equals current output on their own eval set; host it or license weights for edge. Revenue: build fee plus per-video-hour inference or per-camera license. On-prem enterprise later via vendor OEM partners. Durable asset is the pipeline and eval tooling, not Marlin-2B.
