**Claims.** Messy data is the blocker: mostly true (Gartner Nov 2025, 183 finance leaders: AI use flat at 59%; top barriers are data/skills and data quality). Engineers needed: weakening (NetSuite's free MCP connector; one ERP only). "Nobody gives controllers a cleaned cross-system model": false — Maximor ($9M seed; read-only start; "single reconciled source of truth"; 35x revenue growth to multi-million ARR, 25+ customers; one close 11 → 3 days); Concourse ($12M A Jan 2026, a16z and YC; plain English over ERP, billing, CRM; usage pricing); FloQast ($200M ARR, 3,500+ customers, no-code agent builder March 31, 2026).

**Who owns what.** ERPs own data (NetSuite reconciliation agent, AI bank matching 2026.2, free connector). Close vendors own budget (BlackLine $700M revenue, 4,394 customers; FloQast; Numeric $51M B). Ramp owns spend data at smaller companies (controller/AP/bookkeeping agents, Feb 2026). Rivals: Maximor, Concourse, YC's Alder, FullSeam, Zalos, End Close. No one owns a cross-system model yet.

**1. Strongest version.** Keep the compiler; aim at companies running several ERPs/ledgers (PE roll-ups, multi-entity mid-market, high payment volume). Ship three finished agents: bank/processor-to-GL matching, intercompany reconciliation, duplicate-payment and vendor fraud screening. Agent-building is expansion; read-only sold as the advantage (week-long security review, audit/SOX story). Sell via PE operating partners.

**2. Ratings.** Need 4 (Gartner barriers; Maximor growth and 11→3 day close). Value 2 (Maximor already sells the reconciled source of truth and posts entries; FloQast builder; free NetSuite AI access; only multi-ERP open). Market 4 (BlackLine $700M + FloQast $200M ARR; larger labor pool). Risk 2 (customized ERPs make the compiler consulting; read-only caps value; incumbents ship agents quarterly).

**3. Kill and test.** Kill: the compiled model isn't repeatable (founder-written mapping per customer) while controllers get "good enough" from NetSuite, FloQast or Ramp. Test: 10 controllers at multi-entity/PE companies with ≥3 systems; paid 30-day pilot delivering last month's bank/processor-to-ledger exceptions from read-only access; log engineering hours. Pass if ≥3 pay within 2 weeks and onboarding is under ~10 engineering hours each.
