# Merged generator output, 2026-09-24 (three independent generators)

1. **Arc flash studies from equipment photos** (generators 1 and 3, found independently).
   - NFPA 70E/70B require an arc flash study every 5 years and after any system change.
   - A study costs $3.5k-$50k. Collecting the equipment data (photos, nameplates, redrawing the one-line diagram) is 40-60% of the effort.
   - Product: a technician photographs the panels, a vision model builds the one-line diagram and the electrical model, and exports it to ETAP, SKM or EasyPower. A licensed PE stamps the study.
   - Competitors: no AI-native one found. ETAP's app needs typed-in data; REALTIMEais manages data but doesn't read photos; CIMA+ has an internal model that classifies fuses at 91% accuracy.
   - Growth: the NFPA 70B maintenance program, then a live electrical model for multi-site owners, data centers and insurers.
   - Risks: partly a services business with PE liability; the purchase repeats only every 5 years; Schneider may own ETAP; photos can't show hidden wiring; the team has no EE license.
   - Market (generators' own estimate): $250M-$1.5B a year in study spend.

2. **Long-term-care Medicaid applications for nursing homes** (generator 2). Generator 1 dropped it, citing CoreCare and ExaCare.
   - From January 2027, retroactive coverage is cut from 3 months to 2, so each slow filing costs about $9,450 a month.
   - Applications need 60 months of bank records. The AI reads the transactions, flags transfers, fills the state form and drafts replies to caseworkers.
   - Indiana owed 496 nursing homes $462M in delayed payments.
   - Competitors: Telos LTC (Texas, form software), CoreCare (tracking only), Richter (services).
   - Market estimate: $0.4B-$0.6B a year.

3. **Warranty claim recovery for RV, boat, powersports and ag-equipment dealers** (generator 1). Generator 3 dropped dealer warranty, citing Forge AI, ClaimLane and WickedFile.
   - Paid as a share of money recovered.
   - One top-50 RV dealer found $45k of unfiled claims in 60 days.
   - WarrCloud has raised $40M, serving car dealers only.
   - Elkhart, the RV manufacturing hub, is near campus.
   - Risks: a small market per vertical; fees of 2-4% of claims are the norm.

4. **Credit-report dispute triage for lenders and collectors** (generator 1).
   - Complaints to the CFPB doubled to 6.6M in 2025, 88% of them about credit reporting, driven by AI-written disputes. Furnishers must investigate each dispute within 30 days.
   - No dedicated startup found.
   - Risks: a policy change could shut off the dispute flood; bank sales are slow.

5. **Accessible STEM course materials for public universities** (generator 3). Generators 1 and 2 dropped it as crowded.
   - The WCAG 2.1 AA deadline is April 2027.
   - The DOJ says automated tools fail on STEM content.
   - Purdue has an in-house tool, ink2html.
