Desk research only. This is not customer validation.

The control median is 11 (the mean is 11.33). Under the tie rule adopted the same evening, arc flash and disputes advance and Medicaid stops. All six verdicts were PASS. The scores are in `scores.csv`.

Judges' strongest versions and fastest tests:
- **Arc flash.**
  - Strongest version: sell to independent NETA testing firms at a per-site annual price. Photos taken on each 70B maintenance visit update the last model and flag changed breakers and settings.
  - Fastest test: take one firm's photo archive and stamped model from three completed studies, time the engineer reviewing our export against normal re-keying, then ask for a paid next study.
  - Kill if review is not at least 40% faster or the firm won't pay $500.
  - Main risk: ETAP (Schneider) adds photo extraction to its free field app. Competitors: eGalvanic ($1.9M) and arcflash.ai.
- **Disputes.**
  - Strongest version: bureau (e-OSCAR) disputes only, as certified middleware. It matches each dispute to account and ownership documents, deletes when documents are missing, builds a litigation file and scores risk.
  - Fastest test: a paid 30-day pilot at $2 per dispute offered to 5 subprime lenders and 5 debt buyers.
  - Kill if fewer than 2 sign, typical cost is under $1, or the answer is "we just delete."
  - Main risks: Bridgeforce; Salient ($60M) expanding into disputes.
- **Medicaid.**
  - Outsourced firms (Medicaid Done Right, 620+ facilities) and CoreCare (YC, inside PointClickCare) cover it.
  - Fastest test: sort 100 closed pending cases by cause of delay.
