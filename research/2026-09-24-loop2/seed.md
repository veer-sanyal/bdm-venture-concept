> Seed: build on Jev, a new kind of AI model from TypeSafe AI (launched 2026-09-15; docs at docs.typesafe.ai). Jev does not generate text. You send it a state (text or JSON, up to about 32k tokens) and a set of typed questions: yes/no, pick one of up to 255 options, or a score on a rubric of 2 to 10 levels you define. It answers every question in one pass with probabilities, in roughly 0.25 to 0.5 seconds, at $0.042 per million input tokens with output free, which is about $17 per million small decisions. Its output always matches the schema. Independent tests from its first nine days: it trails frontier LLMs by roughly 6 to 12 points on hard labelling tasks and is level with mid-price LLMs; splitting one judgment into several small questions and combining them lifted one task from 63% to 95% accuracy; sending only low-confidence items to an LLM matched LLM quality at a quarter to half the cost; calibration is good on familiar tasks and off on unfamiliar ones, so thresholds need labelled outcomes. Known weaknesses: no arithmetic, counting or date comparison, weak multi-step reasoning, answers shift with option order and with text injected into the state, English works best, API only with no fine-tuning, and every customer gets the same model. Early use is mostly integrations and demos (grading LLM output, risk checks on agent tool calls, feed filtering, instant categorisation); no named production customers yet. Look for a company that Jev makes possible and that was not practical before it.
