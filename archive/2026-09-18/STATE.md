### The five rules this session added, all now in METHOD

- **THE COMPLEMENT TEST (Part 2c).** Incumbents commoditize their complements. Ask **whose core revenue
  does this make more valuable** before writing a candidate down. If it names a large incumbent, it dies.
- **FOUR ESCAPES, ONE DEMOTED.** Platform cannot be neutral · platform legally barred · party opposed to

### Casebook shapes found this session
**The two-sided buyer** (selling to both sides of a dispute means no customer) · **the unenforceable
guarantee** (a contract drafted so the counterparty never owes anything makes evidence of breach
worthless) · **the pincer** (where a remedy exists a regulator runs it free, where none exists the
evidence buys nothing, and there is no middle band) · **the impeaching study** (a free academic result on
your exact question does not compete with your pool, it impeaches it - GMU found homes near datacenters
sell for MORE) · **convergence is not evidence** (two blind agents agreeing tells you about the generator,
not the world; both instances of the convergent candidate died).

### ⚠⚠ H7 POST-CHECK, 2026-09-11 (night). THE LEAD CANDIDATE DOES NOT SURVIVE AS SCOPED. Full file: `H7-LICENSING-CHECK.md`

**1. The licensing bar is real, current, and squarely on the product as scoped.**
**CBP HQ H350722, January 16, 2026, signed Yuliya A. Gulis, is a real ruling** and the paraphrase omitted
its most load-bearing sentence: *"A tool does not constitute a 'person' as defined by 19 C.F.R. § 111.1,
such that the actual decision regarding the classification of imported merchandise... must be made by a
duly licensed customs broker."* It also holds that **buying the model from a third party does not cure it**,
and 19 CFR 111.3(a) requires customs business be conducted inside US customs territory.

**And the threshold is POSSIBILITY, not intent or use. HQ 115248 (2001), fetched:** customs business arises
*"when the possibility exists that corrected classification information derived from the verification
process will end up on the entry."* The losing party there was a wholly-owned sister subsidiary. **The
corporate-compliance exclusion is closed by its own text** (a "related business entity" needs >50%
ownership and the exclusion *"does not extend to the actual preparation or filing of the documents"*), so
it cannot reach an arm's-length vendor.

**What H350722 DOES bless is the commoditized product:** a general classification database usable
*"regardless of whether [a] particular product is ever actually imported,"* operating separately from any
entry workflow, with a meaningfully implemented disclaimer. **That is GingerControl, MIC, Descartes and
ONESOURCE, and it discards the entire differentiated claim.** M0: a mutation exists and it lands in the
dead zone, which is the same answer as no principled mutation.

**STAGE 6: WHAT THE MODEL ACTUALLY DECIDES.** Written 2026-09-07 at Veer's instruction, against the
three questions in `METHOD.md` stage 6. **Recorded as a claim to be tested, not as a verified finding.**

**1. What does the model decide?** *Given this chargeback's line items and this supplier's own
production, inspection and shipping records for the lot in question, it decides which lines to contest,
on what ground, with which document, and which to concede.* **It is a triage and evidence-selection
decision, not a drafting one.** Drafting the 8D is table stakes and several free tools already do it
(GM's SPPS, Stellantis' Cross-Out Requests). **The judgment being sold is: this $6,200 debit contains a
sort-labour line that is defensible on your shift records, a downtime line that is not defensible at all,
and a premium-freight line that is defensible only if you can produce the carrier's dispatch time. Fight
two, concede one, and here is what to attach.**

**2. Why is that decision only automatable now?** Because the input is a heterogeneous pile of
unstructured documents in a different format per customer, with no schema anywhere: a scanned debit memo,
a third-party sorting firm's PDF report, an emailed 8D template, a photograph of a rejected part, an ERP
export, a supplier quality manual in prose. **A 2015 rules engine needs a schema and there is none to
have.** The specific hard step is reading a third-party sort report and matching its part numbers, shift
times and quantities against the supplier's own production records well enough to say whether the claimed
sort hours are supportable. The second hard step is textual: reading THIS customer's supplier quality
manual and deciding what its language actually obliges. **Both are reading-and-reasoning over
inconsistent documents, which is precisely the class of work that became tractable recently.**

**3. What does the model learn from?** **The disposition, which is returned in writing and is a clean
three-state label.** Piston P-PR200.12 states the outcomes verbatim: *"amend the QPN, rescind the QPN or
let the QPN stand and proceed with processing."* The loop is short and complete: recommendation ->
rebuttal filed -> disposition returned, usually inside weeks -> label. **That is the a16z loop with no
part missing, and it is why this candidate is AI-native rather than a service with a model attached.**

**THE TWO THINGS THAT WOULD BREAK THE LOOP, both already flagged as open:**
- **If the reversal comes back as one negotiated number rather than line by line (rung 5b), the label is
  coarse and the model learns "that dispute half-worked" instead of "that evidence reversed that line."**
  The loop degrades from a graded example to a noisy one.
- **If pooling is barred (rung 5c), the model still learns, but only inside ONE supplier's own history.**
  That is a materially weaker product: useful, defensible on privacy, and no longer a cross-supplier data
  moat. It becomes a good tool rather than a compounding asset. **Note that this is survivable, unlike
  5b, and the pitch would have to change rather than end.**

**Day one versus later, stated plainly so the cold start is not hidden.** Customer #1 buys triage,
evidence selection and a filed rebuttal inside their window, against zero accumulated history, using only
their own records and the customer's own published manual. That is real work and it is worth money
because a busy quality manager currently either misses the window or attaches the wrong thing. **The
cross-plant claim ("downtime lines at this plant reverse when you produce X") cannot be said out loud
until many suppliers who share an OEM are on the system, and pretending otherwise in front of judges is
the fastest way to lose the room.**
